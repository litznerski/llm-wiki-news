# Sudan Conflict Governance Transformation

> [!NOTE] Strategic Mechanism Anchor ("Why")
> - **Core Dynamic**: Systematic dismantling and reconfiguration of subnational administrative and judicial structures by warring factions, replacing traditional Native Administration institutions with centralized ideological and military secretariats to construct parallel state authorities.
> - **Primary Case**: RSF Commander [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]]'s Decree No. 19 of 2026 dissolving civil administrations across Darfur and Kordofan to consolidate authority under [[Tasees]].

---

## 1. Core Thesis & Structural Mechanism ("Why")

The protracted fragmentation of Sudan has accelerated structural governance transformations, evolving from tactical territorial control into competing institutional state-building projects. Both the [[Transitional Sovereignty Council]] in Port Sudan and the [[Rapid Support Forces]] in western Sudan have established parallel administrative apparatuses, taxation bodies, and judicial courts. In RSF-controlled regions, the initial reliance on local tribal and Native Administration leaders has been dismantled in favor of centralized political structures like [[Tasees]] (Civil Administration Foundation) to ensure absolute military-political command over civilian enclaves.

---

## 2. Historical Roots & Political Economy Anatomy

Native Administration (Idara Ahliyya) historically provided the bedrock of local dispute resolution, customary land tenure adjudication (Hawakir), and communal taxation across Darfur, Kordofan, and eastern Sudan. While co-opted under previous authoritarian regimes, traditional tribal chiefs (Nazirs, Omdas, and Sheikhs) retained substantial communal legitimacy. During the 2023–2026 war, the RSF initially leveraged native leaders to recruit tribal fighters and legitimize local civil control; however, persistent tribal dissent, resistance to forced recruitment, and disputes over toll revenues prompted RSF high command to eliminate autonomous native authority.

---

## 3. Operational Manifestations Across Frontlines

- **Decree No. 19 of 2026**: On 31 October 2026, Gen. [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]] issued an executive decree dissolving all existing regional civil administrations in RSF-held Darfur and West Kordofan states, terminating the tenures of established civil committees and transferring all administrative and fiscal authority to central [[Tasees]] secretariats.
- **Suppression of Independent Traditional Leadership**: RSF security branches arrested or sidelined recalcitrant traditional leaders who refused to surrender municipal revenues or mobilize youth for frontline battles.
- **Port Sudan Administrative Centralization**: Concurrently, the SAF-led government in Port Sudan reconstituted ministerial headquarters, appointed military officers as state governors, and formed joint emergency committees to enforce wartime civil conformity.

---

## 4. Humanitarian, Regional & Diplomatic Consequences

- **Erosion of Customary Conflict Resolution**: The abolition of native civil administrations removes the primary traditional mechanism capable of de-escalating inter-communal friction, grazing disputes, and localized clashes.
- **De Facto Partition and Parallel Sovereignty**: The establishment of distinct tax codes, identification mechanisms, and administrative bureaucracies deepens the territorial and institutional partition of Sudan between east and west.
- **Complications for Multilateral Aid Delivery**: International humanitarian agencies are confronted with navigating competing legal frameworks, conflicting registration demands, and harassment from parallel authorities when attempting to move aid across frontline borders.

---

## 5. Empirical Debates, Uncertainties & Competing Analyses

The RSF portrays the restructuring as an anti-corruption reform designed to modernize local government, eradicate nepotism, and establish inclusive civilian administration through [[Tasees]]. Conversely, human rights monitors, Darfuri civil society, and traditional tribal councils view the move as an authoritarian seizure aimed at subordinating local populations, extracting wartime revenue, and silencing community leaders who oppose RSF hegemony.
