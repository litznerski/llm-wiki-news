# Ethnic Cleansing and Genocidal Patterns in Darfur

> [!NOTE] Structural Mechanism Anchor ("Why")
> - **Core Dynamic**: The recurrence of state-sponsored and paramilitary-led identity-based extermination, mass killings, sexual violence, and forced demographic displacement targeting non-Arab ethnic communities (Fur, Zaghawa, Masalit) in western Sudan.
> - **Primary Finding**: Formal determination by the [[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]] (February 2026) that RSF actions during the siege and takeover of [[El Fasher]] bore the **hallmarks of genocide**.

---

## 1. Core Thesis & Structural Mechanism ("Why")

The violence perpetrated in Darfur during the 2023–2026 conflict represents an acute escalation and structural continuation of the genocidal campaigns of 2003–2005. The [[Rapid Support Forces]]—descended from the Janjaweed tribal militias—have systematically combined military operations against the SAF with coordinated ethnic terror against historically non-Arab indigenous populations. The operational objective is the demographic clearance and political subjugation of non-Arab strongholds to secure uncontested ethnic, economic, and territorial control over the Darfur region.

---

## 2. Historical Roots & Political Economy Anatomy

Darfur's ethnic fault lines were weaponized under the regime of Omar al-Bashir, which mobilized Arab nomadic pastoralist militias against sedentary African farming communities (Fur, Masalit, Zaghawa). The creation of the RSF under Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo (Hemedti) institutionalized this militia structure into a formal state-backed force. In 2023, the resumption of war provided an opportunity to finish the demographic transformation of key urban centers, beginning with the massacre of the Masalit in El Geneina (`SD06`) in 2023, followed by the siege of [[El Fasher]] in 2024–2025.

---

## 3. Operational Manifestations Across Frontlines

- **The 500-Day Siege of El Fasher**: For 18 months, the RSF encircled [[El Fasher]], cutting off all incoming food, water, and medical convoys, deliberately inflicting conditions of life calculated to destroy the civilian population.
- **Statutory Acts of Genocide**: The UN Fact-Finding Mission established that during the late October 2025 takeover, the RSF committed mass killings, inflicted serious mental and bodily harm, and carried out selective sexual violence targeting Zaghawa and Fur women while sparing Arab women.
- **Racialized Perpetrator Rhetoric**: Eyewitness accounts recorded fighters declaring: *"Is there anyone Zaghawa among you? If we find Zaghawa, we will kill them all"* and *"We want to eliminate anything black from Darfur."*

---

## 4. Humanitarian, Regional & Diplomatic Consequences

- **Catastrophic Displacement & Famine**: Produced the displacement of over 500,000 survivors into Jebel Marra and Chad, solidifying IPC Phase 5 Famine across displacement sites.
- **Heightened Legal Obligations**: FFM Chair [[Mohamed Chande Othman]] stressed that where evidence indicates genocide, the international community has a heightened legal obligation under the 1948 Genocide Convention to prevent, protect, and hold perpetrators accountable.

---

## 5. Empirical Debates, Uncertainties & Competing Analyses

RSF leadership consistently issues denials, attributing violence to rogue elements or tribal clashes. However, the UN Fact-Finding Mission determined that the scale, coordination, and senior command endorsement make genocidal intent "the only reasonable inference" from the systematic pattern of conduct.
