## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

Haiya is a strategic transport and energy junction in [[Red Sea State]] (`SD10`), situated approximately 200 km southwest of [[Port Sudan]]. It sits at the bifurcation of the national railway network—linking Port Sudan with Atbara, Khartoum, and Kassala—and hosts a major pumping station along the pipeline transporting crude oil from southern oilfields to the Bashayer export terminal on the Red Sea coast.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

- **SAF Hinterland**: Held continuously by the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] and state security forces throughout the war.
- **Vulnerability to Long-Range UAV Attacks**: In late October 2026, Haiya became the target of repeat long-range strike drones operated by the [[Rapid Support Forces]], penetrating eastern Sudanese airspace to hit energy infrastructure.

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Oil Pipeline Pumping Station**: Pumping station serving the main pipeline transporting crude oil to coastal export terminals; sustained heavy damage with crude storage tanks set on fire on 31 October 2026.
- **Electrical Transformer Substation**: Regional high-voltage power distribution substation (under construction) targeted in repeat UAV strikes.
- **Railway & Road Junction**: Principal transit corridor for commercial freight and humanitarian logistics between eastern ports and the interior.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

While spared from ground combat, Haiya experiences disruptions in electricity transmission and fuel logistics resulting from drone strikes on energy infrastructure. State authorities tightened security protocols and civil monitoring following strikes in October 2026.
