# Netiga

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Agrarian & Pastoralist Crossroads in South Darfur
> **Netiga** (`نتيقة` / `SD03004`) is an agricultural and livestock locality in northeastern [[South Darfur]], located north of Nyala along routes leading toward Al Fasher and East Darfur. Home to mixed agrarian and pastoralist communities, Netiga serves as a crucial grain and livestock supplier for urban consumption in Nyala.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Under de facto control of the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) since late 2023 following the fall of Nyala, Netiga is administered under the RSF-backed [[Tasees]] regional structure.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Weekly Grain and Cattle Markets**: Regional nexus for trading millet (*dukhn*), sorghum (*dura*), and groundnuts.
- **Livestock Pastoral Grazing Belts**: Traditional wet-season and dry-season pastoralist corridors.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Hyperinflation & Collapsing Terms of Trade**: In November 2026, grain prices skyrocketed (millet reaching 400,000 SDG/sack) while cattle prices collapsed due to parched pastures and liquidity absence, pushing rural households into IPC Phase 4 (Emergency) conditions.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2026-11-01**: Field market reports document hyperinflation in basic staples and a severe livestock market crash in Netiga.
