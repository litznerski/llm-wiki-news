---

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

El Fasher is the historical capital of the Darfur region and the provincial capital of [[North Darfur]] (`SD02`). Historically hosting the headquarters of the joint UN-AU peacekeeping mission (UNAMID), the city served as the final stronghold of the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] and the allied [[Joint Darfur Force]] in western Sudan before its fall in late October 2025. It commanded vital overland trade corridors to Libya, Chad, and Central African Republic.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

- **Prolonged Siege (May 2024 – October 2025)**: For over 500 days, the [[Rapid Support Forces]] and allied Arab militias laid siege to El Fasher, encircling the SAF 6th Infantry Division garrison and Joint Force battalions led by Minni Minawi and Gibril Ibrahim.
- **Fall of the City (October 2025)**: The RSF launched a coordinated multi-pronged assault that overran military bases, triggering mass displacement, targeted executions, and sexual violence.
- **Genocidal Findings**: On 19 February 2026, the UN Fact-Finding Mission concluded that the RSF committed underlying acts of genocide against Zaghawa and Fur civilians during and following the takeover.

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Air & Road Corridors**: El Fasher Airport and the highway linking to Nyala, El Geneina, and Omdurman.
- **Healthcare & Water Infrastructure**: Saudi Hospital, South Hospital, and municipal boreholes were systematically shelled and destroyed during the siege.
- **Humanitarian Camps**: Adjacent to [[Zamzam IDP Camp]], Abu Shouk, and Al-Salam camps, hosting hundreds of thousands of displaced non-Arab civilians.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

Under IPC Phase 5 Famine thresholds, the civilian population endured catastrophic starvation, lack of medical supplies, and acute water deprivation deliberately imposed by the siege. Tens of thousands of survivors fled toward Tawila and Jebel Marra.
