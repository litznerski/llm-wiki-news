## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

Nyala is Sudan's second-largest city by population after Greater Khartoum and the primary commercial metropolis of western Sudan. Located at the terminus of the national railway line and hosting Nyala International Airport, it serves as the command headquarters, logistics hub, and political nerve center of RSF-controlled territory in Darfur, where the paramilitary force established a parallel civilian administration.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

- **RSF Capture (October 2023)**: Following heavy fighting that destroyed the SAF 16th Infantry Division headquarters, the RSF consolidated uncontested territorial control over Nyala.
- **Aerial Bombardment & Drone Target**: Because of its role as an RSF logistics hub, Nyala has been repeatedly subjected to long-range airstrikes and unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) attacks conducted by the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]]. On 27 October 2026, a mass-casualty SAF drone strike hit Tanqonj Market, killing at least 102 civilians.

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Nyala International Airport**: Heavily utilized for military and logistical operations; alleged landing site for external cargo flights.
- **Tanqonj Market & Souq al-Jabal**: High-density commercial congregation centers providing livelihoods for local residents and displaced communities.
- **Railway Terminus**: Critical heavy freight railway terminus linking Darfur to Kordofan and eastern Sudan.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

Nyala hosts a massive displaced population scattered across Otash and Kalma camps. While markets maintain trade flows from South Sudan and the Central African Republic, civilian populations face extreme price inflation, public service collapse, and recurring air strike trauma.
