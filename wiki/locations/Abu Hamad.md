# Abu Hamad

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Gold Hub & Nile Bend Crossroads
> **Abu Hamad** (`أبو حمد` / `SD04006`) is a major commercial locality and riverine junction town located at the sharp northeastern bend of the Nile in [[River Nile State]]. Serving as the logistical supply base for the sprawling Nubian Desert artisanal gold mines, Abu Hamad is an economic linchpin supporting the mineral trade, currency circulation, and overland fuel transit between Atbara and Merowe.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Under continuous SAF territorial administration, Abu Hamad hosts security forces protecting regional mineral resources and Nile transport infrastructure.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Abu Hamad Power Substation**: Connects regional gold processing facilities and residential districts to the national electricity grid.
- **Gold Markets & Refining Smelters**: Primary procurement market where gold prospectors exchange raw nuggets for cash and equipment.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- Plunged into a full municipal blackout following RSF drone attacks on 1 November 2026, shutting down water treatment plants and cooling for medical supplies.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2026-11-01**: Three suicide drone detonations strike the Abu Hamad electricity substation, causing a total blackout across the city.
