---

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

El Obeid is the capital of [[North Kordofan]] State (`SD13`) and the preeminent commercial, agricultural, and logistics hub connecting Greater Khartoum to the western Darfur states and southern Kordofan. With an estimated wartime population of approximately 600,000 residents—swelled by internally displaced persons (IDPs) from across Kordofan and Darfur—the city commands critical highway intersections, the western railway terminus, and the strategic El Obeid Airport. Controlling El Obeid is vital for securing supply corridors between central Sudan and the western frontlines.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Since the outbreak of the war in April 2023, El Obeid has been a heavily contested fortress city:
- **SAF Defensive Stronghold**: The [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] 5th Infantry Division (popularly known as the *Hajanah* or Camel Corps) maintains fortified control over the central city, military headquarters, and administrative buildings.
- **Paramilitary Auxiliaries**: The [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]] and allied Islamist militias maintain billets, checkpoints, and interrogation facilities in residential areas (e.g. Al-Muwazzafeen).
- **RSF Encirclement**: The [[Rapid Support Forces]] maintain a semi-permanent blockade around the city's perimeter, cutting off primary access roads to Khartoum, Darfur, and South Kordofan.
- **Air & Drone War Escalation**: In October 2026, the UN Fact-Finding Mission documented that RSF strike drones repeatedly penetrated urban airspace, hitting residential neighborhoods, displacement shelters, and utilities, while SAF forces positioned heavy weapons and billets within residential areas, increasing civilian risk.

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Logistics & Transport**: Crossroads of the Western Salvation Highway and national rail network.
- **Gum Arabic & Commodity Markets**: Historically the world's largest exchange market for gum arabic (*Acacia senegal*), groundnuts, sesame, and livestock.
- **Healthcare Infrastructure**: Only ~30% of health facilities in El Obeid remain functional. The El Obeid Teaching Hospital, El Obeid Maternity Hospital (damaged 16 June 2026), and Kuwaiti Specialized Children's Hospital have faced severe structural damage, power blackouts, and critical supply shortages.
- **Water & Electricity Grids**: The central water pipeline network from Bara and deep urban boreholes have been repeatedly severed by shelling, forcing civilians to rely on untreated water carting amidst active cholera outbreaks.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

According to the October 2026 report by the [[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]] (A/HRC/65/CRP.1), El Obeid's civilian population faces severe deprivation, siege-like shortages, and escalating state repression.
- **Custodial Repression & Security Cells**: SAF military intelligence, GIS, and [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]] operationalize emergency "Security Cells." Over 2,000 detainees are held in El Obeid Central Prison (built for 450), crammed 35–40 to a 2.5×2m cell with 200 sharing a single latrine. Detainees endure physical torture, electric shocks, and denial of medical care ([[Arbitrary Detention and Custodial Torture in the Sudan War]]).
- **Civic Space Contraction**: Systematic surveillance, arbitrary detentions, and harassment targeting Emergency Response Rooms (ERRs), lawyers, and local volunteers ([[State Surveillance and Civic Space Contraction in Sudan]]).
- **Healthcare & Nutrition**: Medical facilities operate near total exhaustion due to fuel shortages, damaged electrical grids, and blockaded medical supply lines.
