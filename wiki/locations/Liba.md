# Liba

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Mountain Foothill Settlement & Displacement Hub in East Jebel Marra
> **Liba** (`ليبة` / `SD02008-LIB`) is a mountain foothill settlement and the administrative seat of the Liba Federation, one of eight federations comprising [[Dar Omo]] in East [[Jebel Marra]]. Under the territorial governance of the [[Sudan Liberation Movement (al-Nur)]] (SLM-AW), Liba hosts over 14,000 residents and internally displaced persons (approximately 2,415 families) seeking shelter from Darfur's frontlines.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Governed peacefully by SLM-AW civil and native authorities, Liba features no active military installations from either the SAF or RSF. Displaced populations are overseen by a local Displaced Persons and Organizations Committee led by [[Adam Yagoub Mohamed Abdallah]]. Surrounding settlements and camps include Kankli (`مخيم كانكلي`), Bale Sereif (`مخيم بلي سيريف`), and Feina (`مخيم فينة`).

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Liba Health Center**: The sole regional medical outpost serving Liba and roughly 30 surrounding mountain villages; rendered non-functional for over 10 months due to complete medicine and staff stockouts.
- **Unprotected Wadi Wells**: Drinking water supply derived from unprotected shallow hand-dug wells in the wadi stream bed, alongside a single motorized pump producing brackish, saline water.
- **Liba Elementary & Intermediate School**: Regional educational facility suffering dramatic student dropouts due to extreme poverty.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Acute Famine & Waterborne Epidemics**: The 95% crop failure of 2026 left families without food reserves. Drinking contaminated wadi water caused widespread outbreaks of malaria, respiratory infections, severe diarrhea, and anemia.
- **Aid Agency Evacuation**: All international and domestic humanitarian NGOs previously operating in Liba withdrew, leaving host families completely destitute.
