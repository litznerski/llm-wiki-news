# Um Baru

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Mountainous Borderland Locality
> **Um Baru** (`أم برو` / `SD02006`) is a rural locality in western [[North Darfur]], situated between [[Karnoi]] to the north and Kutum to the southeast. Characterized by rugged arid valleys and rocky hills, Um Baru lies along strategic routes utilized by civilian refugees and commercial truckers fleeing violence in El Fasher toward Chad.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Garrisoned historically by the SAF and the [[Joint Darfur Force]], Um Baru has been encircled and repeatedly attacked by the [[Rapid Support Forces]] seeking to eliminate resistance movement staging grounds.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Internal Displacement Corridors**: Feeder route connecting displaced persons from Kutum and Kebkabiya toward Tina.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Ongoing Displacement Outflows**: Between 29 and 31 October 2026, armed clashes and militia raiding displaced an estimated 1,150 civilians across four rural villages, forcing flight into Chad.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2026-10-31**: IOM confirms displacement of 1,150 civilians from four villages in Um Baru locality.
