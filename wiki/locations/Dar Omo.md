# Dar Omo

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Eastern Jebel Marra Mountain Administrative Circle
> **Dar Omo** (`دار أمو` / `دائرة أمو` / `SD02008-OMO`) is a rugged administrative circle in East Jebel Marra, straddling the transition between the mountainous volcanic slopes of the [[Jebel Marra]] massif and the eastern Darfuri plains. Comprising eight local federations—including [[Liba]], Bale, Soro, Kila, Liba Kadneer, Feina, Kara, and Lugudlu—Dar Omo falls under the sovereign civil administration of the [[Sudan Liberation Movement (al-Nur)]] (SLM-AW).

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Under continuous SLM-AW administration, Dar Omo is administered by traditional native authorities, prominently headed by Shartai [[Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah]]. The area has maintained neutrality in the war between the SAF and RSF, establishing it as a primary haven for displaced persons escaping the siege of [[El Fasher]] and violence in Shangil Tobaya.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Mountain Valley Corridors**: Valley passages linking East Jebel Marra with North and South Darfur markets.
- **Seasonal Wadi Agriculture**: Historically reliant on rain-fed terraced farming, mountain streams, and seasonal wadis for staple grain and onion cultivation.
- **Extreme Remoteness & Blockade**: Encircling RSF forces across the lowlands prevent commercial fuel, seeds, and medical supplies from ascending into the circle.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Acute Famine & 95% Agricultural Collapse**: In October 2026, native authorities warned of catastrophic imminent famine following a "white autumn" (`خريف أبيض`) that yielded only 5% of normal seasonal precipitation.
- **Health Infrastructure Failure**: Essential medical facilities have ceased operation, with the single central health center non-functional for over 10 months, prompting surges in malaria, respiratory illness, and anemia.
