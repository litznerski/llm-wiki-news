# North Kordofan

> [!NOTE] State Geopolitical Anchor
> - **Canonical Name**: North Kordofan (`Shamal Kurdufan` / `ولاية شمال كردفان`)
> - **Administrative Level**: `state_adm1` (`ADM1`), Federal Republic of Sudan
> - **UN OCHA P-Code**: `SD13`
> - **State Capital**: [[El Obeid]] (`SD13001`)
> - **Current Control**: Contested — [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] maintain the capital [[El Obeid]] and recaptured northern corridors (Bara, Jabrat al-Sheikh), while the [[Rapid Support Forces]] dominate western localities (Sodari, Al-Mazroub, [[Um Badr]]) and surrounding transit routes.
> - **IPC Acute Food Insecurity**: **Phase 4 (Emergency)** across rural localities and displaced camps.

---

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

North Kordofan is a central state acting as the strategic terrestrial pivot between the Nile Valley and the Darfur region. Bordering Northern State, Khartoum, White Nile, South Kordofan, and North Darfur, its territory controls vital livestock corridors, agricultural production belts, and the primary road artery linking Port Sudan and Khartoum to the west.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

- **Divided Control & Shifting Corridors**: The [[Rapid Support Forces]] rapidly seized control of major rural localities and highway checkpoints early in the war, surrounding [[El Obeid]].
- **Mid-to-Late 2026 Offensive Fluctuations**: In late July 2026, SAF operations briefly recaptured the Bara and Jabrat al-Sheikh corridors to open supply lines to El Obeid. However, RSF counteroffensives recaptured Sodari on 25 September 2026 and Al-Mazroub on 2 October 2026, re-establishing an operational stranglehold over northern and western transit corridors.
- **Siege Dynamics & War Crimes**: Throughout 2024–2026, the state became a theatre of attritional blockade and long-range UAV operations, with RSF drone strikes hitting military and civilian infrastructure inside El Obeid, while RSF units conducted summary executions of captured and bound civilians in western localities such as [[Um Badr]] ([[2026-09-24 Um Badr Market Summary Executions]]).

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Western Salvation Highway**: The vital logistics lifeline linking Khartoum with Kosti, El Obeid, and El Fasher.
- **Agricultural Production**: Dominant center for gum arabic, sesame, groundnuts, and camel/cattle trading.
- **Water Infrastructure**: Rural rain-fed reservoirs (*hafirs*) and urban pipe systems severely compromised by fighting and power cuts.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

Widespread displacement into [[El Obeid]] has strained food supplies and municipal infrastructure. The October 2026 UN Fact-Finding Mission report highlighted severe humanitarian deprivation, exacerbated by blockades on aid convoys imposed by both belligerents, alongside systematic custodial torture and arbitrary detentions in El Obeid Central Prison ([[Arbitrary Detention and Custodial Torture in the Sudan War]]).
