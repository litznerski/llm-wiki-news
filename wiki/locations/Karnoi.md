# Karnoi

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Northwestern Darfur Borderland
> **Karnoi** (`كرنوي` / `SD02007`) is a remote border locality in northwestern [[North Darfur]], bordering Chad to the west, Um Baru to the south, and Tina to the northwest. Anchoring the historic homeland of the Zaghawa community, Karnoi is a critical transit corridor connecting Darfur’s interior to Chadian supply bases in Tina and Iriba.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Traditionally secured by joint Darfuri armed movements (including JEM and SLM factions forming the [[Joint Darfur Force]]), Karnoi has faced persistent harassment and raiding by RSF mobile units attempting to isolate Zaghawa strongholds.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Cross-Border Smuggling & Relief Corridors**: Vital unpaved desert tracks connecting eastern Chad to rural North Darfur.
- **Traditional Pastoral Wells**: Strategic desert water points essential for pastoralist migration.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Acute Civilian Displacement**: In late October 2026, armed attacks displaced over 850 villagers from Kamo and Touki into Tina and across the Chadian border.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2026-10-31**: Armed insecurity forces 850 civilians to flee Kamo and Touki in Karnoi locality.
