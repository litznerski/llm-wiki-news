# El Malha

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Northern Desert Oasis & Trans-Saharan Trade Lifeline
> **El Malha** (`المالحة` / `SD02008`) is a remote volcanic oasis locality in northeastern [[North Darfur]], centered around the striking Malha crater lake. Bordering Northern State to the northeast and Libya to the north, El Malha is an essential desert transit hub connecting Darfuri commerce to Kufra and the Nile Valley, hosting vital weekly livestock and goods markets.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Traditionally an area dominated by the Meidob community, El Malha maintained relative autonomy through local defense agreements and [[Joint Darfur Force]] patrols. However, both the SAF and RSF have contested transit routes around the locality, with belligerents launching drone attacks against desert vehicle convoys.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Weekly Livestock & Produce Market**: Central commercial nexus where desert pastoralists exchange camels and livestock for imported grain and commodities.
- **Trans-Saharan Trucking Corridors**: Vital lifeline for informal trade bypassing besieged towns like El Fasher.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Severe Drought & Crop Failure**: Faced catastrophic agricultural failure and severe water shortages in late 2026, forcing thousands of families to sleep in the open under trees.
- **Vulnerability to Aerial Strikes**: Remote commercial vehicles traveling to market face recurrent drone missile strikes.

- **2026-11-01**: A drone strike destroys a commercial transport vehicle near Al-Hara, killing two named civilians.
