# Khartoum Oncology Hospital

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] National Cancer Treatment Center
> **Khartoum Oncology Hospital** (مستشفى الخرطوم لعلاج الأورام), historically known as *Mustashfa al-Dharra* (Radiation and Isotopes Centre Khartoum - RICK), is Sudan’s primary specialized public cancer referral facility, located in central [[Khartoum]] (`SD01001`). Serving patients from all 18 states, it historically provided over 80% of Sudan’s radiation therapy, nuclear medicine, and pediatric oncology protocols.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Located near intense urban frontline sectors in central Khartoum, the hospital has suffered severe shell damage, repeated looters' raids, and staff evacuations during the 2023–2026 war. Despite these risks, volunteer oncologists and nursing teams led by Director-General [[Ahmed Omar]] have fought to maintain outpatient clinics and chemotherapy dispensing.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Radiotherapy Equipment Halls**: Built to house linear accelerator bunkers and cobalt-60 teletherapy units, currently non-operational due to physical damage, lack of replacement parts, and electrical volatility.
- **Chemotherapy Reconstitution Laboratory**: The sole specialized oncology pharmacy remaining partially functional in Khartoum.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Devastating Equipment Deficit**: Totally lacks functional radiotherapy devices, forcing thousands of cancer patients into terminal complications.
- **Electrical Outages**: Dependent on emergency diesel generators that frequently shut down due to nationwide grid strikes and fuel scarcity.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2023-05-10**: Caught in crossfire between SAF and RSF, forcing partial evacuation of pediatric cancer wards.
- **2026-11-01**: Concluded Pink October awareness month with an urgent national appeal for a linear accelerator and power grid restoration.
