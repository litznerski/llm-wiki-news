# Ad Douiem

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Commercial & Academic Center of Northern White Nile
> **Ad Douiem** (`الدويم` / `SD16003`) is the second-largest urban center in [[White Nile State]], situated on the western bank of the White Nile approximately 190 kilometers south of Khartoum. Historically renowned for the Bakht er Ruda educational institute, Ad Douiem functions as a pivotal commercial crossroads linking northern White Nile agricultural zones with the Kosti-Rabak urban corridor.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Under the control of the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF), Ad Douiem has served as a sanctuary zone for hundreds of thousands of civilians fleeing combat in Khartoum and Al Jazirah. However, its proximity to RSF movements along the western desert axis and Gezira borders has rendered it vulnerable to asymmetric utility attacks, particularly strikes disabling the regional electrical grid at the Mashkoor substation.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Mashkoor Substation Grid Link**: Receives high-voltage transmission lines from the Mashkoor transformer facility, powering municipal water stations, mills, and hospitals across Ad Douiem, El Kawa, and Wad az Zaki.
- **Bakht er Ruda University & Hospitals**: Key regional tertiary education and referral health facilities.
- **Nile River Crossing & Trade Highway**: Critical western bank transport artery facilitating commodity trade between central Sudan and South Sudan.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Severe Infrastructure Breakdown**: Following the destruction of the Mashkoor substation on 26 October 2026, Ad Douiem suffered total municipal blackouts.
- **Market Hyperinflation**: Lack of refrigeration caused ice block prices to surge from 10,000 to 30,000 SDG, bread production collapsed by over 60%, and solar-charging kiosks imposed heavy financial tolls on local households.
