# Northern State

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Strategic Rear Depth & Northern Trade Gateway
> **Northern State** (`الولاية الشمالية` / `Ash Shamaliyah` / `SD05`), with its capital at Dongola, encompasses Sudan's northwestern Nile basin, bordering Egypt to the north, Libya to the northwest, River Nile State to the east, and North Darfur and North Kordofan to the south and west. Throughout the 2023–2026 war, Northern State served as a premier strategic rear redoubt and logistical gateway for the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF), anchoring cross-border commerce with Egypt via the Argeen and Qustul crossings.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Northern State has remained under firm SAF military governance since April 2023, garrisoned by the 19th Infantry Division in Merowe and the 73rd Infantry Brigade in Al Dabbah, reinforced by allied volunteer auxiliary units, notably the [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]]. However, starting in late 2024 and intensifying through late 2026, the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) leveraged long-range drone capabilities to launch deep-strike attacks against power grids, fuel depots, and military command posts across Merowe and Al Dabbah.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Merowe Dam & Hydropower Complex**: Sudan's largest hydroelectric facility, supplying baseload electricity to central and eastern states.
- **Wadi Halfa Border Corridors**: Vital road and ferry transit routes linking Sudan to Aswan, Egypt.
- **Agricultural Schemes**: Intensive irrigated farming along the Nile riverbanks producing winter wheat, dates, and legumes.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **IDP Sanctuary**: Absorbed over 500,000 internally displaced persons fleeing Khartoum and Gezira.
- **Infrastructure Vulnerability**: Drone attacks on transformer stations in Al Dabbah and Merowe have triggered repeated state-wide power outages, disabling irrigation pumps and hospital operations.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2023-04-15**: Clashes erupt around Merowe Airport between SAF and RSF at the war's outbreak.
- **2026-11-01**: Coordinated RSF drone strikes target Al Dabbah power station and 73rd Brigade base, killing Al-Baraa commander Muhammad al-Nao.
