---

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

Assosa is the regional capital of Benishangul-Gumuz in western Ethiopia, situated less than 50 kilometers from the international boundary with Sudan's [[Blue Nile State]] (`SD17`). The city hosts Assosa Airport and regional military installations proximate to the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD). Its proximity to southeastern Sudan makes it a strategic geographic platform for cross-border logistics.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Under sovereign Ethiopian military and federal police jurisdiction. In late 2026, Sudanese diplomatic and military intelligence briefings alleged that the base had been repurposed by foreign sponsors—principally the [[United Arab Emirates]]—as a logistical conduit to resupply RSF units in Blue Nile, Sennar, and Kordofan.

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Assosa Military Airfield & Logistics Hub**: Capable of handling transport aircraft and unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) ground control stations.
- **Cross-Border Corridors**: Terrestrial roads leading into Kurmuk and Geissan in [[Blue Nile State]].

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

Not located within the active Sudanese conflict zone, but its alleged utilization for armed supply operations impacts cross-border refugee dynamics and relations between Sudanese and Ethiopian border communities.
