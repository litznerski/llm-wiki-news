# West Kordofan

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!IMPORTANT] Geopolitical Location Anchor (`LOCATION_ANCHOR`)
> - **Canonical Name**: **West Kordofan** (`غرب كردفان` / *Gharb Kurdufan*)
> - **UN OCHA P-Code**: **`SD18`** | **Administrative Level**: `ADM1` (`state_adm1`)
> - **Key Localities & Hubs**: El Fula (state capital), Babanusa (rail and military junction), **Dibab**, **Ghubeish**, **El Nuhud**, and the southern border zone adjacent to [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`).
> - **Strategic Significance**: Critical land bridge connecting Darfur to central Sudan, housing major oilfields and transit corridors contested between the [[Rapid Support Forces]] and the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

- **[[Rapid Support Forces]] Control in Dibab**: As documented by [[OHCHR]] in October 2026, the town of **Dibab** in [[West Kordofan]] is under the territorial control of the [[Rapid Support Forces]] ([[2026-10-18 Dibab Double-Tap Drone Strike]]).
- **Aerial Interdiction by [[Sudanese Armed Forces]]**: While ground arteries across much of [[West Kordofan]] are held or contested by the [[Rapid Support Forces]], the state is heavily targeted by long-range UAV strikes, including strikes between 6 and 10 October 2026 in **Ghubeish** and **El Nuhud** attributed by [[OHCHR]] to [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] ("military government forces") ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]]).

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Overland Transport Corridors**: Commercial and passenger transport trucks crossing through **Dibab**, **El Nuhud**, and **Ghubeish** serve as the primary supply lifelines between Kordofan, Darfur, and the [[Abyei]] border market at Amiet. On 18 October 2026, a transportation truck in Dibab was hit in a repeat "double-tap" drone strike that killed at least 139 people ([[2026-10-18 Dibab Double-Tap Drone Strike]]).
- **Oil & Cross-Border Trade Links**: Borders the oil-producing, demilitarized special administrative area of [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`), where the [[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]] devastated cross-border trade.

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- **Attacks on IDP Shelters**: Between **6 and 10 October 2026**, alleged [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] drone strikes hit shelters housing internally displaced persons (IDPs) in **Ghubeish** and **El Nuhud**, killing **at least 25 civilians**, including women and children ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]]).
- **Mass Casualty Trauma & Service Collapse**: [[OHCHR]] spokesperson [[Seif Magango]] reported that relentless drone bombardment in [[West Kordofan]] has killed scores of civilians and displaced persons while eviscerating vital civilian infrastructure ([[2026-10-23 Seif Magango Statement on Evisceration of Civilian Infrastructure in Sudan]]).
