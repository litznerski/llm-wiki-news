# Al Dabbah

## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

> [!NOTE] Strategic River Bastion & Cross-Desert Forward Staging Hub
> **Al Dabbah** (`الدبة` / `Ed Debba` / `SD05004`) is a strategic river bend city and locality headquarters in southern [[Northern State]]. Situated along the Nile between Merowe and Dongola, Al Dabbah anchors the northern terminus of the trans-desert road connecting Northern State to North Darfur (via El Malha) and North Kordofan. It hosts major military bases guarding the southern approaches to Dongola.

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

Garrisoned by the SAF 73rd Infantry Brigade, Al Dabbah also serves as a primary forward operating and recruitment base for the allied Islamist [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]]. It has faced repeated RSF drone incursions aimed at severing military supply links.

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Central Electricity Distribution Facility**: Supplies electrical power to southern Northern State and riverine agricultural pumps.
- **73rd Infantry Brigade Headquarters**: Command center coordinating regional defense and trans-desert patrols.
- **Al-Multaqa Commercial & Fuel Depots**: Major highway refueling and transit junction.

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

- Suffered critical infrastructure damage and power disconnection following the 1 November 2026 drone strikes.

## 5. Chronological Log of Major Conflict Events

- **2026-11-01**: Coordinated drone strikes hit the central electricity distribution facility, 73rd Brigade base, and Al-Multaqa fuel depots, killing Al-Baraa commander [[Muhammad al-Nao]].
