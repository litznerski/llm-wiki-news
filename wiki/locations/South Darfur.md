## 1. Geopolitical Identity & Strategic Significance

South Darfur borders Central Darfur, East Darfur, South Kordofan, and internationally borders the Central African Republic and South Sudan. It is historically the most populous and economically dynamic state in Darfur, anchoring major cattle export corridors, cash-crop agriculture, and border trade networks into Central Africa.

---

## 2. Territorial Control History & Armed Presence

- **Complete RSF Domination**: Following the fall of [[Nyala]] in October 2023, the [[Rapid Support Forces]] established uncontested military and administrative hegemony across all state localities (Kas, Beliel, Edd al-Fursan, Rehed al-Birdi).
- **SAF Airstrikes**: The state has been targeted extensively by SAF fixed-wing aviation and long-range strike drones seeking to interdict RSF military formations and supply lines.

---

## 3. Key Infrastructure, Trade Corridors & Resources

- **Border Trade Corridors**: Terrestrial routes linking Nyala southward to Um Dafaq (CAR border) and Raja (South Sudan).
- **Agricultural & Livestock Wealth**: Significant livestock populations, groundnut, and sorghum production.
- **Urban Transport**: Rail and road infrastructure centered on [[Nyala]].

---

## 4. Humanitarian Status, Displacement & Civil Services

Hosts some of Darfur's largest long-standing IDP settlements, including Kalma and Otash. Healthcare, clean water, and banking services have largely collapsed, replaced by informal hawala remittances and emergency community initiatives.
