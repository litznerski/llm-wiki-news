# Darfur Victims Support Organization

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> The **Darfur Victims Support Organization** (`منظمة مناصرة ضحايا دارفور`) is an independent grassroots human rights and forensic advocacy organization dedicated to monitoring, documenting, and publishing real-time civilian casualty rolls, airstrikes, and atrocities perpetrated against civilians across the five Darfur states.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Formed by Darfuri legal advocates, community monitors, and displaced activists, the organization operates an extensive network of local observers stationed across vulnerable rural towns, IDP camps, and transport nodes.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Victim Identification & Naming**: Restoring dignity to non-combatant casualties by recording their legal names, ages, and circumstances of death.
- **Forensic Airstrike Tracking**: Documenting the use of indiscriminate aerial bombs and combat drones against civilian transport vehicles and weekly village markets.
- **Universal Accountability**: Submitting verified documentation to UN bodies, the ICC, and international human rights syndicates.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Deep field access across remote northern Darfur localities including El Malha, Karnoi, Um Baru, and Mellit.
