# Nafisa Hagar

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Vice President of the Darfur Bar Association & International Law Specialist
> **Nafisa Hagar** (`نفيسة حجر`) is a leading Sudanese human rights lawyer and the Vice President of the [[Darfur Bar Association]] (DBA). Known for her rigorous documentation of mass atrocities, wartime sexual violence, and arbitrary detentions, Hagar specializes in the application of International Humanitarian Law (IHL) and the 1949 Geneva Conventions to the Sudan conflict.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

As a senior executive in the Darfur Bar Association, Hagar directs legal defense teams and coordinates international filings with the ICC and United Nations human rights treaty bodies.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Enforcing IHL and the Geneva Conventions**: Establishing that the indiscriminate use of explosive weapons and suicide drones in populated areas constitutes war crimes with individual criminal liability.
- **Protecting Women and Children**: Exposing how women and children bear the heaviest mortality and social disintegration burdens of the war.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Prepares formal evidentiary submissions for the UN Fact-Finding Mission and international tribunals.
- Strong alliances with the [[No to Women's Oppression Initiative]] and [[Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights]].
