# Hamdan Suleiman

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> **Hamdan Suleiman** (`حمدان سليمان`) is a Sudanese civilian resident of the Doumaya (`حي دوماية`) neighborhood in [[Nyala]] (`SD03001`), South Darfur. He provided crucial forensic eyewitness testimony to international wire agencies (AFP) and [[BBC News]] Arabic following the 27 October 2026 double-tap drone strike on the Tanqonj / Dumaya marketplace, in which his 19-year-old son was killed.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Suleiman is a non-combatant civilian whose personal testimony provided vital ground-truth corroboration of mass informal burials and the extreme scale of civilian fatalities in Nyala.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Truth-Telling and Bereavement Record**: Providing unvarnished testimony on the human toll of aerial drone strikes and the necessity of burying dozens of loved ones in communal mass graves when municipal mortuary capacity collapses.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Direct participant in communal emergency burial efforts in Nyala during late October 2026.
