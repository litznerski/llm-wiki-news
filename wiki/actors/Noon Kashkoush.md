# Noon Kashkoush

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Director of Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights & Accountability Advocate
> **Noon Kashkoush** (`نون كشكوش`) is a prominent Sudanese lawyer, civil society leader, and the Director of the [[Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights]]. Kashkoush has gained international recognition for her frontline advocacy documenting atrocities, village blockades, and weapon transfers fueling violence across central Sudan.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating from legal defense circles and coordinating independent human rights investigators across Al Jazirah and Khartoum, Kashkoush leads civil society coalitions demanding legal accountability for both domestic armed actors and foreign sponsor states.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Banning Weaponized Drones**: Demanding an immediate global embargo and operational cessation of explosive drone usage in residential sectors.
- **Accountability for Foreign Enablers**: Explicitly targeting foreign states providing weaponry, surveillance technologies, and logistic support to Sudanese belligerents.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Extensive network of grassroots investigators monitoring civilian impacts in Al Jazirah and surrounding states.
- Close legal partnership with the [[Darfur Bar Association]].
