## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

As UN High Commissioner for Human Rights at the head of [[OHCHR]] in Geneva, **[[Volker Türk]]** directs UN human rights field monitoring teams covering the Sudan conflict, working alongside [[OHCHR]] spokesperson [[Seif Magango]] and UN peacekeeping missions such as [[UNISFA]].

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Cessation of External Arms Transfers & Civilian Protection**: In his 23 October 2026 statement, [[Volker Türk]] emphasized that peace efforts in Sudan must directly confront the continuous influx of weapons fueling UAV warfare and press for a halt to arms transfers used in manifest disregard of international humanitarian law ([[2026-10-23 Volker Türk Condemnation of Sudan Drone Escalation and Arms Influx]], [[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]).

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **UN Human Rights Verification Mandate**: Issues formal determinations on conflict mortality trends—confirming in October 2026 that drone strikes now account for the majority of conflict-related civilian deaths in Sudan—and calls on both [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] and [[Rapid Support Forces]] to commit to an immediate ceasefire.
