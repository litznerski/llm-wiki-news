# Dino Mahtani

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Forensic Investigator & Sanctions Specialist (`wiki/actors/Dino Mahtani.md`)
> **Dino Mahtani** is an investigative analyst, author, and veteran forensic researcher who served as an official United Nations Security Council Sanctions Panel investigator covering illicit finance, transnational smuggling, and armed group funding across East Africa and the Great Lakes. Prior to his UN service, he was a senior correspondent for the *Financial Times*, *Reuters*, and the *BBC*.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

- **Investigative Trajectory**: Built a decades-long career analyzing armed conflict political economies, starting as an international correspondent in West, Central, and East Africa before appointment to the UN Sanctions Panels.
- **UN Sanctions Mandates**: Served on UN Security Council monitoring groups (notably the Somalia and Eritrea Monitoring Group / SEMG), investigating sanctions violations, weapons trafficking, and the commercial revenue streams of armed factions including al-Shabaab.
- **Advisory & Think-Tank Work**: Later served as Deputy Director for Africa at the International Crisis Group (ICG), directing policy research on regional security dynamics across the Sudans, the Horn of Africa, and the Sahel.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Evidentiary Independence & Anti-Impunity**: Advocates rigorous, field-verified documentation of illicit transnational flows and the complicity of regional sovereign powers in sustaining armed conflicts.
- **Scrutiny of Sanctions Evasion**: Highlights the mechanisms through which Gulf financial and logistics hubs (specifically the UAE) facilitate sanctions circumvention, money laundering, and resource exploitation (such as charcoal and conflict gold) at the expense of regional stabilization.
- **Multilateral Institutional Integrity**: Critiques the co-optation of the [[United Nations]] by wealthy sovereign actors, warning that trading diplomatic posts for sovereign investment compromises international justice.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Forensic Field Investigation**: Experienced in supply-chain tracing, corporate registration analysis, satellite imagery verification, and covert site identification in hostile environments.
- **Direct Engagement with Sovereign Delegations**: Possesses direct first-hand experience interacting with the UAE Mission to the UN during the tenure of Ambassador [[Lana Nusseibeh]], documenting patterns of procedural obfuscation and denial.
- **Target of State Retaliation**: Subjected to state retaliation for his investigative findings, including arbitrary detention and deportation by UAE state security while in transit through the UAE in 2022.

## 5. Chronological Event Footprint & Accountability Record

- **2013–2014**: Documented over 160 illicit shipments of Somali charcoal entering the UAE in violation of UN Security Council resolutions, locating and photographing warehouses in the UAE desert despite denials from the UAE UN Mission under [[Lana Nusseibeh]].
- **2022**: Detained, interrogated, and expelled by UAE authorities upon airport transit, an action directly tied by legal inquiries to his past exposure of UAE sanctions evasion networks.
- **2026-10-28**: Authored a major forensic exposé in [[Middle East Eye]] opposing the appointment of [[Lana Nusseibeh]] as UN Secretary-General, detailing her record of denying sanctions violations and warning that appointing an Emirati official would destroy the UN's moral credibility amidst the Sudan war ([[2026-10-28 Dino Mahtani Statement on UAE Sanctions-Busting and Lana Nusseibeh UNSG Bid]]).
