# Nile Petroleum Company

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] National Fuel Logistics & State Petroleum Distributor
> The **Nile Petroleum Company** (`شركة النيل للبترول`) is a leading Sudanese state-owned downstream petroleum distribution enterprise operating under the regulatory supervision of the Ministry of Energy and Petroleum. The company manages strategic fuel storage reserves, pipeline distribution terminals, and retail fueling stations across government-held northern and eastern states.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Established as a cornerstone of Sudan's sovereign domestic hydrocarbon logistics, Nile Petroleum operates strategic depots supplying diesel and gasoline to civilian transportation, regional agricultural schemes, and SAF military formations.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Fuel Security**: Guaranteeing continuous supply of refined fuels for municipal power generators, commercial trucking corridors, and military logistics.
- **Strategic Reserves**: Maintaining emergency hydrocarbon storage depots in rear hubs such as [[Shendi]] and [[Atbara]].

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Extensive network of bulk fuel storage tank farms, tanker truck fleets, and retail distribution points.
- Closely integrated into national civil defense and defense logistics networks.
