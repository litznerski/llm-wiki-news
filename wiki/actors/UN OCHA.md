# UN OCHA

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> The **United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs** ([[UN OCHA]] / *أوتشا*) is the United Nations Secretariat entity responsible for bringing together humanitarian actors to ensure a coherent response to emergencies. In Sudan, OCHA coordinates the inter-agency Humanitarian Response Plan, leads negotiations for humanitarian access across frontlines and borders, and publishes authoritative field situation reports and infrastructure damage assessments.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Following the eruption of the war in April 2023, OCHA established its operational headquarters in Port Sudan while maintaining cross-border operational hubs in Chad and South Sudan.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Humanitarian Access Negotiation**: Securing cross-line and cross-border corridors (e.g., Adré crossing) to deliver emergency relief into famine-declared zones.
- **Critical Infrastructure Monitoring**: Systematically tracking damage to electrical grids, water treatment plants, and hospitals under international humanitarian law.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Cross-Line Coordination & Access Mandate**: Maintains formal diplomatic and logistical channels across both SAF-controlled administrative areas and RSF-controlled western territories to facilitate humanitarian relief convoys.
- **Inter-Agency Telemetry & Incident Tracking**: Deploys satellite analysis, field monitors, and joint cluster reporting systems to track displacement waves, facility strikes, and famine indicators across all 18 states.
