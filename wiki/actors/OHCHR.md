## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Headquartered in Geneva, **[[OHCHR]]** is the mandated United Nations body responsible for monitoring, verifying, and reporting violations of international human rights and international humanitarian law (IHL) in the Sudan conflict. Its public findings are presented by High Commissioner [[Volker Türk]] and spokesperson [[Seif Magango]] and disseminated via [[UN News]] ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Civilian Protection & IHL Accountability**: [[OHCHR]] investigates indiscriminate aerial attacks, "double-tap" drone strikes, and attacks on protected civilian objects (hospitals, markets, IDP shelters), while advocating for an immediate ceasefire and a halt to external arms transfers fueling IHL violations in Sudan ([[2026-10-23 Volker Türk Condemnation of Sudan Drone Escalation and Arms Influx]]).

---

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Forensic Casualty Verification**: Cross-verifies conflict fatalities across [[West Kordofan]], [[Blue Nile State]], [[Abyei]], Darfur, and Khartoum State in coordination with UN field missions such as [[UNISFA]].
