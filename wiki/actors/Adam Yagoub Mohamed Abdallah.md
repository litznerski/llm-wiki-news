# Adam Yagoub Mohamed Abdallah

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Grassroots Displacement Coordinator & Humanitarian Focal Point
> **Adam Yagoub Mohamed Abdallah** (`آدم يعقوب محمد عبد الله`) is the head of the Displaced Persons and Organizations Committee for the [[Liba]] Federation (`مسؤول لجنة شؤون النازحين والمنظمات بفيدرالية ليبة`), located within [[Dar Omo]] in East [[Jebel Marra]]. Serving as the principal grassroots humanitarian focal point for thousands of displaced persons sheltering in the mountain haven, Abdallah compiles field telemetry on food insecurity, health infrastructure collapse, and school attrition.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating under the civilian coordination framework sanctioned by the [[Sudan Liberation Movement (al-Nur)]] (SLM-AW) and native administration leadership, Abdallah interfaces directly with village elders, displaced families, and regional relief organizations.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Humanitarian Witness**: Raising global awareness of the catastrophic conditions confronting over 14,000 residents and IDPs in Liba following the complete withdrawal of aid agencies.
- **Health and Education Defense**: Advocating for the emergency rehabilitation of Liba's defunct health center and financial support to prevent massive child school dropouts.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Direct oversight of relief coordination, water well management, and census tracking for 2,415 families in Liba.
- Strong coordination with Shartai [[Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah]].
