# Sudanese Teachers Committee

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> The **Sudanese Teachers Committee** (STC / `لجنة المعلمين السودانيين`) is an independent trade union and professional syndicate representing primary and secondary school educators across Sudan. Founded in resistance to the co-opted official syndicates of the Bashir regime, the STC was a founding component of the Sudanese Professionals Association (SPA) which spearheaded the December 2018 Revolution.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating through an elected Executive Bureau and a decentralized Higher Strike Committee with coordinators in every school district, the STC organizes mass collective bargaining, strikes, and legal advocacy for educator welfare.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Public Education Defense**: Demanding that state budgets prioritize education over military and security apparatus expenditures.
- **Living Wage & Arrears Settlement**: Fighting chronic wage devaluation, hyperinflation, and multi-month delays in public salary disbursement.
- **Professional Dignity**: Protecting educators from arbitrary dismissals, political vetting, and security harassment.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **National Strike Coordination & School Walkouts**: Maintains communication channels across municipal education departments to mobilize simultaneous labor stoppages and sit-ins.
- **Revolutionary Syndicate Alliances**: Operates as a core member of the Sudanese Professionals Association (SPA), collaborating with doctors, engineers, and neighborhood resistance committees.
