# International Organization for Migration

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> The **International Organization for Migration** ([[International Organization for Migration]] / IOM / *منظمة الهجرة الدولية*) is the leading intergovernmental organization within the United Nations system dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration. In Sudan, IOM operates the **Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM)**, which provides the primary international empirical telemetry on internal displacement, population movements, and cross-border refugee flows.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating in Sudan since 2000, IOM scaled up nationwide field monitoring following the outbreak of large-scale hostilities in April 2023, deploying thousands of enumerators across all 18 states and border entry points.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Empirical Displacement Verification**: Rigorously documenting the exact scale, geographic origin, and demographic profile of displaced households to guide international emergency relief operations.
- **Cross-Border Telemetry**: Monitoring refugee crossings into Chad, South Sudan, Egypt, and Ethiopia.
- **Direct Emergency Assistance**: Distributing shelter kits, clean water, and non-food items to displaced families.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) Infrastructure**: Operates a nationwide network of key informants, community enumerators, and satellite verification analysts providing real-time displacement logs.
- **Multilateral Humanitarian Partnerships**: Works in close operational alignment with UNHCR, [[UN OCHA]], and international donors to allocate emergency response funds.
