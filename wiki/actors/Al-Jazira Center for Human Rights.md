# Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Grassroots Documentation & Human Rights Monitoring Center
> The **Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights** (`مركز الجزيرة لحقوق الإنسان`), directed by lawyer and human rights advocate [[Noon Kashkoush]], is an independent civil society organization dedicated to investigating atrocities, extrajudicial killings, village raids, and civilian infrastructure destruction in Al Jazirah State and across central Sudan.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Formed in response to mounting human rights catastrophes and civilian massacres during the war in central Sudan, the center brings together local attorneys, grassroots monitors, and youth activists to record field violations under acute security threats.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Halting Asymmetric Drone Proliferation**: Demanding immediate international bans on weaponized drone transfers to Sudanese combatants.
- **Arms Supplier Accountability**: Publicly naming and demanding sanctions against foreign states facilitating the flow of lethal drone technology and heavy weaponry into Sudan.
- **Victim Protection**: Advocating for the creation of internationally monitored safe zones and civilian corridors.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Deep grassroots investigative links across central agricultural towns and displacement settlements.
- Solid analytical partnerships with the [[Darfur Bar Association]] and regional feminist anti-war coalitions.
