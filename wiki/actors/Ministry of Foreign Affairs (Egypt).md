# Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Egypt)

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Egypt)** (`وزارة الخارجية المصرية`) oversees the foreign relations, bilateral diplomacy, and regional security policies of the Arab Republic of Egypt. Given Egypt’s shared 1,270-kilometer southern border, historical Nile Valley union, and core national security interests centered on the Nile waters and Red Sea stability, the Egyptian MFA plays a central, influential role in regional diplomacy surrounding the Sudan conflict.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Egypt has historically aligned closely with the formal state institutions of Sudan and the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF). Cairo convened the Sudan Neighboring States Summit in July 2023 and has hosted numerous consultative rounds bringing together Sudanese political factions and civil society.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Preservation of Sudanese State Institutions**: Opposing any parallel statelets or dissolution of the regular armed forces, viewing the SAF as the guarantor of Sudanese state integrity.
- **Interdiction of Cross-Border Proxy Arms**: Demanding that regional neighbors halt the transit and logistical funneling of advanced weaponry (including strike drones and MANPADS) to the [[Rapid Support Forces]].
- **Regional Stability**: Mitigating refugee spillover and preventing hostile regional proxy encirclement along its southern flank.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Diplomatic Clout & Multilateral Leverage**: Leverages non-permanent membership mechanisms at the UN Security Council, the African Union Peace and Security Council, and the Arab League to advocate for Sudanese state sovereignty.
- **Logistical & Humanitarian Support**: Provides vital humanitarian corridors, consular processing at Wadi Halfa and Aswan, and medical assistance across northern Sudan.
