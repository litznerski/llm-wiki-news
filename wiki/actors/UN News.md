## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

**UN News** operates under the United Nations Department of Global Communications in New York and Geneva, aggregating official field reports, peacekeeping situational updates, human rights investigations, and Security Council communiqués across multilingual desks (English, Arabic, French, and others) ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

As the official journalism and public-information platform of the United Nations system, [[UN News]] documents international humanitarian law (IHL) compliance, civilian casualties verified by UN human rights monitors ([[OHCHR]]), and peacekeeping operations such as [[UNISFA]] in [[Abyei]].
