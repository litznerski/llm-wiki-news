---

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Mandated by the United Nations Security Council, **[[UNISFA]]** is the sole authorized military force permitted inside the disputed, oil-rich border region of [[Abyei]] between Sudan and South Sudan. Its operational structure includes quick-reaction peacekeeping contingents—such as the Pakistani battalion commanded by Lt. Col. [[Muhammad Farooq]]—and the [[UNISFA]] Level-II/III field hospital located in **Todach** under the overall command of Acting Force Commander Brig. Gen. [[Adebowale Ajabge]] ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Demilitarization & Civilian Protection**: [[UNISFA]] is mandated to maintain [[Abyei]] as a weapon-free zone, protect civilians under imminent threat of physical violence, facilitate humanitarian aid, and provide emergency trauma response during cross-border conflict spillovers.

---

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Quick Reaction, Firefighting & Medical Evacuation**: During the [[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]], [[UNISFA]] peacekeepers were the first responders on the scene, deploying firefighting teams to contain market fires and evacuating more than 27 injured civilians to the [[UNISFA]] hospital in Todach ([[2026-10-23 Muhammad Farooq First-Responder Account of Amiet Market Fire]], [[2026-10-23 Adebowale Ajabge Report on UNISFA Emergency Response and Todach Evacuations]]).
