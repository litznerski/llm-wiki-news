# Ihsan Fagiri

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Pioneering Sudanese Feminist Leader, Medical Doctor & Rights Defender
> **Dr. Ihsan Fagiri** (`د. إحسان فقيري`) is a veteran Sudanese physician, prominent feminist leader, and human rights defender. As a leading figure in the [[No to Women's Oppression Initiative]] and former chairperson of the Doctors' Union executive committee, Dr. Fagiri has spent decades organizing against military dictatorship, state oppression, and gender-based violence in Sudan.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Arrested and detained multiple times during both the al-Bashir regime and post-2021 military rule for her civil disobedience, Dr. Fagiri commands immense respect across Sudan's democratic and trade union movements. She continues her advocacy through cross-border feminist alliances.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Anti-War Advocacy**: Uncompromising opposition to both warring military apparatuses (SAF and RSF), centering the catastrophic bodily and social toll borne by Sudanese women.
- **Protection of Essential Infrastructure**: Condemning the weaponization of drones against electricity, water, and health services that sustain civilian existence.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Mobilizes Sudanese civil society, medical networks, and international feminist coalitions.
- Close cooperation with the [[Darfur Bar Association]] and [[Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights]].
