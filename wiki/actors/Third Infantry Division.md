# Third Infantry Division

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] SAF Riverine Garrison & Northern Capital Shield
> The **Third Infantry Division** (`الفرقة الثالثة مشاة - شندي`) is a major operational formation of the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF), headquartered in the historic military garrison town of [[Shendi]] in southern [[River Nile State]]. Securing the northern approach corridors to Khartoum, the division defends critical supply lines connecting the capital basin to [[Atbara]], [[Abu Hamad]], and [[Port Sudan]].

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Formed as one of the SAF's foundational territorial divisions, the 3rd Infantry Division maintains extensive military installations across Shendi Locality, including base depots, air defense batteries, and artillery units. Its command reports directly to the General Staff in Port Sudan.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Northern Defence Bastion**: Shielding River Nile State and northern population centers from western and southern RSF incursions.
- **Logistics Protection**: Securing trunk roads, fuel storage depots of the [[Nile Petroleum Company]], and high-voltage transmission lines powering central Sudan.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Integrated infantry brigades supported by heavy artillery and point air-defense systems.
- Coordinates regional security alongside the [[Shendi Locality Security Committee]] and federal intelligence services.
