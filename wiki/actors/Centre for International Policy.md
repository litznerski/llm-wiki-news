# Centre for International Policy

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Independent Foreign Policy & Human Rights Think Tank (`wiki/actors/Centre for International Policy.md`)
> The **Centre for International Policy** (CIP) is an independent, non-profit research and advocacy organization founded in 1975 and headquartered in Washington, D.C. Directed by foreign policy scholars and human rights advocates, CIP promotes a demilitarized, diplomacy-first international policy grounded in human rights, anti-corruption, and accountability for authoritarian regimes.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

- **Founding Context**: Established in 1975 by former diplomats and peace activists in the aftermath of the Vietnam War to promote government transparency and examine the human costs of US foreign and military policies.
- **Organizational Architecture**: Operates specialized programs analyzing security assistance, arms sales, transnational illicit finance, and conflict resolution across the Global South.
- **Leadership & Governance**: Governed by a board of veteran diplomats, legal scholars, and civil society leaders, emphasizing independent non-partisan research free of foreign government financing.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Demilitarizing Foreign Policy**: Advocates curbing unconditioned military assistance and weapons transfers to states engaged in systemic human rights abuses or proxy warfare.
- **Anti-Kleptocracy & Financial Transparency**: Conducts in-depth research on how authoritarian regimes and Gulf petrostates leverage sovereign wealth, lobbying firms, and financial centers to buy political influence and insulate themselves from accountability.
- **Defending Multilateral Integrity**: Works to preserve international institutions—principally the [[United Nations]]—from institutional capture by autocratic governments seeking to whitewash war crimes and domestic repression.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Policy Analysis & Advocacy**: Produces high-impact policy briefs, congressional testimonies, and coalition statements engaging the US Congress, the UN Secretariat, and international bodies.
- **Civil Society Coalitions**: Partners closely with international human rights watchdogs, including [[Human Rights Watch]], Amnesty International, and regional civil society organizations across Africa and the Middle East.
- **Funding Integrity**: Refuses funding from foreign governments and defense contractors to preserve investigative impartiality.

## 5. Chronological Event Footprint & Accountability Record

- **2024–2026**: Published investigations and statements urging stricter oversight on US arms transfers and export controls to regional actors fueling the war in Sudan, highlighting the UAE's role in supplying the [[Rapid Support Forces]].
- **2026-10-28**: Issued a formal institutional statement strongly opposing the nomination of Emirati diplomat [[Lana Nusseibeh]] for [[United Nations]] Secretary-General, warning that her career owes itself to an "authoritarian regime that serves as a haven for global kleptocracy and corruption" ([[2026-10-28 Centre for International Policy Statement on Lana Nusseibeh UN Candidacy]]).
