# Embassy of France in Sudan

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> The **Embassy of France in Sudan** (`سفارة فرنسا في السودان`) is the official diplomatic mission representing the French Republic to Sudan. Operating in diplomatic coordination from Paris and regional hubs following the evacuation of Khartoum in April 2023, the French embassy plays an active diplomatic role in European and UN mediation initiatives, co-hosting the Paris International Humanitarian Conference for Sudan and its Neighbours in April 2024.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating under the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs (Quai d'Orsay), the embassy coordinates French humanitarian contributions and multilateral diplomatic initiatives within the UN Security Council and the European Union.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Enforcement of International Humanitarian Law**: Publicly demanding respect for the Geneva Conventions and protection of civilian infrastructure.
- **Support for Democratic Transition**: Reaffirming that there is no military solution to the conflict and demanding unhindered humanitarian access.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **European & Multilateral Diplomatic Weight**: Operates within the EU Foreign Affairs Council and as a permanent member of the UN Security Council (P5) to sponsor sanctions and arms embargo extensions.
- **Humanitarian Donor Mobilization**: Directs French state bilateral assistance and co-convenes international donor conferences supporting Sudanese civil society and humanitarian NGOs.
