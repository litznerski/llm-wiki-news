# Darfur Bar Association

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Independent Human Rights & Forensic Legal Bar
> The **Darfur Bar Association** (`هيئة محامي دارفور` / DBA), with key leadership including Vice President [[Nafisa Hagar]], is an independent, non-governmental legal association founded in 2003 during the initial Darfur genocide. The association provides pro bono legal defense, monitors arbitrary detention, and conducts field documentation of war crimes, crimes against humanity, and violations of international humanitarian law across Sudan.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating continuously through two decades of armed conflict in Darfur, the DBA maintains an extensive decentralized network of defense lawyers, paralegals, and forensic investigators. While forced into exile across Kampala and Nairobi following the outbreak of the 2023 conflict, it retains deep underground documentation networks inside Sudan.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Universal Legal Accountability**: Submitting forensic evidence dossiers to the International Criminal Court (ICC), UN Fact-Finding Missions, and regional human rights tribunals.
- **Enforcement of International Humanitarian Law**: Challenging the legality of indiscriminate weaponry, including artillery shelling and suicide drone deployments in residential zones.
- **Individual Criminal Responsibility**: Emphasizing that command-level perpetrators cannot escape prosecution under statutes of limitations.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Prepares detailed legal briefs citing the 1949 Geneva Conventions and Rome Statute.
- Cooperates closely with international human rights organizations, the Sudanese Bar Association, and regional women's rights groups.
