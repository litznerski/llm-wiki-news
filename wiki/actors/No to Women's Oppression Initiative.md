# No to Women's Oppression Initiative

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Grassroots Sudanese Feminist & Anti-War Movement
> The **No to Women's Oppression Initiative** (`مبادرة لا لقهر النساء`), chaired by prominent human rights defender [[Amira Osman]], is a grassroots Sudanese feminist organization founded in 2009 to combat institutionalized state violence, arbitrary public order laws, and systemic discrimination against women. Since the outbreak of war in April 2023, the initiative has spearheaded civil society campaigns documenting wartime sexual violence, displacement crises, and the civilian impacts of aerial and drone bombardment.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Born during the authoritarian regime of Omar al-Bashir in resistance to public order lashings and gender oppression, the initiative played a prominent role in the 2018–2019 Sudanese Revolution. In wartime exile and domestic underground networks, it functions as a democratic collective of activists, lawyers, and community organizers.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Cessation of Indiscriminate Warfare**: Calling for immediate arms embargoes and an unconditional ceasefire under the banner of "Ground Arms" (`أرضاً سلاح`).
- **Civilian Survival Defense**: Exposing how both the SAF and RSF actively target essential civilian infrastructure to render life inside Sudan unviable.
- **Gendered Accountability**: Documenting rape, trafficking, and economic dispossession inflicted upon Sudanese women.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Cross-border human rights documentation networks linking Sudan, South Sudan, Kenya, and Uganda.
- Key partnerships with the [[Darfur Bar Association]], SIHA Network, and independent human rights monitoring centers.
