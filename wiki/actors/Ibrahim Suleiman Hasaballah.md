# Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Traditional Native Administration Shartai of East Jebel Marra
> **Shartai Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah** (`الشرتاي إبراهيم سليمان حسب الله`) is the paramount traditional native administration leader (Shartai) of the [[Dar Omo]] circle in East [[Jebel Marra]]. Overseeing eight federations situated within territory governed by the [[Sudan Liberation Movement (al-Nur)]] (SLM-AW), Hasaballah exercises customary judicial, tribal, and civil governance authority across the eastern slopes of the mountain redoubt.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

As a hereditary Shartai within Darfur's historic native administrative hierarchy, Hasaballah commands deep customary legitimacy among the Fur and affiliated mountain communities. Working alongside the SLM-AW civil wing, he coordinates community welfare, displacement reception, and dispute mediation.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Humanitarian Crisis Mobilization**: Issuing urgent public appeals to domestic and international relief agencies to avert mass starvation in East Jebel Marra.
- **Communal Protection**: Providing safe haven and communal solidarity to thousands of displaced families fleeing military offensives in El Fasher and surrounding plains.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- High traditional authority across the eight federations of Dar Omo: Liba, Bale, Soro, Kila, Liba Kadneer, Feina, Kara, and Lugudlu.
- Strategic coordination with SLM-AW civilian administration led by Abdul Wahid al-Nur.
