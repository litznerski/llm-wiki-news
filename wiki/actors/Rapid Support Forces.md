## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

The **Rapid Support Forces (RSF)** are a powerful paramilitary force commanded by General Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo (*Hemedti*) locked in a nationwide civil war against the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]]. By October 2026, the RSF maintained territorial control over strategic transit localities in [[West Kordofan]]—including **Dibab**—while projecting long-range drone strikes deep into southeastern Sudan, including [[Blue Nile State]] ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]]).

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Disruption of Civilian Infrastructure & Rival Rear Zones**: According to [[OHCHR]], drone strikes attributed to the [[Rapid Support Forces]] in October 2026 targeted both displaced civilians engaged in artisanal gold mining (Abu Shendi, [[Blue Nile State]]) and critical medical facilities (Um Darfa hospital, [[Blue Nile State]]), contributing to the systematic evisceration of civilian services ([[2026-10-23 Seif Magango Statement on Evisceration of Civilian Infrastructure in Sudan]]). On 31 October 2026, [[United Nations]] Secretary-General [[António Guterres]] (via Spokesperson [[Stéphane Dujarric]]) stated that he was appalled by attacks directed against civilians and critical infrastructure, explicitly including **health facilities** ([[2026-10-31 Stéphane Dujarric UNSG Statement Condemning Escalating Drone Attacks in Sudan and Abyei]]).

---

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Strategic UAV & Loitering Munition Arsenal**: Supported by external arms supply lines condemned by UN High Commissioner [[Volker Türk]], the [[Rapid Support Forces]] operate strike drones capable of hitting targets in [[Blue Nile State]], Kordofan, and Khartoum State ([[2026-10-23 Volker Türk Condemnation of Sudan Drone Escalation and Arms Influx]], [[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]).
