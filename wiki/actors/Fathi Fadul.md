# Fathi Fadul

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor
> **Fathi Fadul** (`فتحي فضل`) is a prominent Sudanese political strategist and the official spokesperson for the **[[Sudanese Communist Party]]** (SCP). Speaking on behalf of the party's central leadership, Fadul articulates the SCP's anti-war doctrine, exposes external geopolitical meddling, and delivers critical telemetry on civilian casualties and humanitarian collapse across Sudan.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

A veteran member of the SCP Central Committee, Fadul coordinates public communiques and international political outreach, interfacing frequently with independent Sudanese press outlets, particularly [[Radio Dabanga]].

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy ("Why")

- **Exposing the Technologization of Violence**: Highlighting how advanced drone systems supplied by foreign patrons have transformed the war into an existential threat to civilian urban life.
- **Popular Resistance Over Military Victory**: Advocating for grassroots popular organization to bring down military rule rather than accepting compromised elite power-sharing deals.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- **Party Communications & Media Dissemination**: Commands the SCP's press office, clandestine bulletins, and digital broadcasts to maintain political agitation across the diaspora and domestic underground cells.
- **Civic & Labor Alliances**: Coordinates policy advocacy alongside trade union syndicates, the [[Sudanese Teachers Committee]], and regional anti-imperialist political parties.
