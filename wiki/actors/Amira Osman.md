# Amira Osman

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Chairwoman of the No to Women's Oppression Initiative & Iconic Rights Activist
> **Amira Osman** (`أميرة عثمان`) is an internationally acclaimed Sudanese human rights defender, feminist leader, and Chairwoman of the [[No to Women's Oppression Initiative]]. Renowned as a recipient of the Front Line Defenders Award for Human Rights Defenders at Risk, Osman has spent over fifteen years on the frontlines of grassroots struggle against military authoritarianism, public order laws, and state violence.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Targeted, arrested, and tortured repeatedly under both the al-Bashir regime and the military junta following the October 2021 coup, Osman has consistently led independent civil movements uncompromised by political elite bargains. Following the outbreak of the war in 2023, she helped reconstitute the democratic feminist movement in regional exile while maintaining underground domestic networks.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Unconditional Demilitarization**: Advocating the fundamental revolutionary slogan "Ground Arms" (`أرضاً سلاح`), rejecting the legitimacy of both the SAF and RSF.
- **Defending the December Revolution**: Characterizing the destruction of urban infrastructure and civilian livelihoods as a deliberate counter-revolutionary strategy by military elites.
- **Exposing External Proxy Meddling**: Condemning foreign powers whose financial and technological interventions perpetuate state violence against the Sudanese people.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- High international and pan-African civil society stature, interfacing with regional protection mechanisms and international human rights platforms.
- Strategic coordination alongside the [[Darfur Bar Association]] and [[Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights]].
