## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

**Brig. Gen. [[Adebowale Ajabge]]** serves as the Acting Force Commander of the **United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei** ([[UNISFA]]), exercising military command over multinational peacekeeping contingents in [[Abyei]], including the Pakistani battalion led by Lt. Col. [[Muhammad Farooq]] and the [[UNISFA]] hospital in Todach.
