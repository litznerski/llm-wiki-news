## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

**Lt. Col. [[Muhammad Farooq]]** commands the Pakistani peacekeeping battalion serving within the **United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei** ([[UNISFA]]) under Acting Force Commander Brigadier General [[Adebowale Ajabge]].

- **Immediate Civilian Protection & First-Responder Stabilization**: Leads rapid-reaction peacekeeping deployments inside demilitarized [[Abyei]] to rescue civilians, contain post-strike fires, and secure mass-casualty impact sites.
