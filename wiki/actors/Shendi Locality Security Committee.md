# Shendi Locality Security Committee

## 1. Identity, Classification & Leadership Anchor

> [!NOTE] Municipal Security Executive & Civil Authority
> The **Shendi Locality Security Committee** (`اللجنة الأمنية لمحلية شندي`) is the primary local governing and security executive coordinating body for Shendi Locality in [[River Nile State]]. Comprising the locality executive director, military commanders from the [[Third Infantry Division]], police chiefs, and General Intelligence Service (GIS) representatives, the committee oversees civil defense, essential services, and local defense management.

## 2. Historical Lineage, Factions & Command Structure

Operating under the authority of the River Nile State Security Committee and the Ministry of Federal Governance, the committee maintains direct oversight of public utilities, displaced persons settlements, and civil emergency response units in Shendi.

## 3. Strategic Objectives, Ideology & Political Economy

- **Civil Infrastructure Protection**: Maintaining the functionality of regional utilities, fuel storage, and municipal services amid asymmetric aerial attacks.
- **Crisis Communications**: Issuing official field communiqués and casualty assessments following aerial drone strikes.

## 4. Operational Capabilities, Alliances & Foreign Backing

- Mobilizes local fire brigades, electrical grid repair teams, and emergency relief units.
- Works in close operational synchronization with the SAF [[Third Infantry Division]].
