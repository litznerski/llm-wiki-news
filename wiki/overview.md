## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Theatre Map

By **late October and early November 2026**, the war between the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) and the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) entered an acute phase of asymmetric long-range unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV / drone) warfare, deep-rear infrastructure interdiction, and heightened multilateral accountability scrutiny ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]], [[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]). According to UN High Commissioner for Human Rights [[Volker Türk]] ([[OHCHR]]) and the UN Fact-Finding Mission, drone attacks have become the **leading cause of conflict-related civilian deaths in Sudan in 2026**, with verified fatalities exceeding **510 civilians killed** and hundreds injured during October 2026 alone.

The strategic landscape is marked by five major developments:
1. **Rear-Area Infrastructure Targeting**: On 31 October 2026, long-range UAVs struck Crude Oil Pumping Station 4 and the municipal electrical transmission grid in [[Haiya]] ([[Red Sea State]]), knocking out local power and threatening vital South Sudanese crude transit to [[Port Sudan]] ([[2026-10-31 Haiya Oil Pumping and Power Station Drone Strike]]).
2. **Urban Market Carnage in Darfur & Demilitarized Zones**: Catastrophic civilian massacres occurred in civilian commercial hubs, notably the 27 October airstrike on Tanqonj livestock market in [[Nyala]] ([[South Darfur]]) killing at least **102 civilians** ([[2026-10-27 Nyala Tanqonj Market Drone Strike]]), the 19 October drone strike on Amiet Market in demilitarized [[Abyei]] killing **more than 80 people** ([[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]), and the 18 October "double-tap" strike in Dibab killing **at least 139 people** ([[2026-10-18 Dibab Double-Tap Drone Strike]]).
3. **Fact-Finding Findings of Genocide, Siege Starvation & Custodial Torture**: The [[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]] (FFM) formally warned the UN General Assembly that RSF atrocities in [[El Fasher]] bear hallmarks of genocide against non-Arab communities ([[2025-10 El Fasher Siege and RSF Takeover Atrocities]]), while document `A/HRC/65/CRP.1` verified catastrophic siege conditions, educational and medical strikes, and systemic custodial torture of 2,000+ detainees in [[El Obeid]] Central Prison by SAF-aligned Security Cells, recommending the establishment of a specialized independent court for Sudan ([[2026-10 El Obeid Siege and Drone Warfare Escalation]], [[Arbitrary Detention and Custodial Torture in the Sudan War]]).
4. **Summary Executions in Western Localities**: On 24 September 2026, RSF forces executed 25 bound detainees in [[Um Badr]] public market in North Kordofan, a verified Common Article 3 war crime ([[2026-09-24 Um Badr Market Summary Executions]]).
5. **Proxy Warfare Scrutiny & Multilateral Mobilization**: Global civil society and Sudanese diaspora coalitions mobilized in Geneva and London to oppose the candidacy of UAE diplomat [[Lana Nusseibeh]] for UN Secretary-General, presenting extensive evidence of Emirati weapons and drone corridors sustaining RSF operations ([[2026-10-28 Global Civil Society Briefing Opposing Lana Nusseibeh Candidacy for UN Secretary-General]]). Simultaneously, UN Secretary-General [[António Guterres]] formally declared drone attacks against civilians and infrastructure to be **violations of international humanitarian law** ([[2026-10-31 Stéphane Dujarric UNSG Statement Condemning Escalating Drone Attacks in Sudan and Abyei]]).

## 3. Humanitarian Crisis, Displacement & Infrastructure Telemetry

- **Systemic Targeting of Economic Congregation Points**: Conflict actors have shifted strikes from purely military garrisons to critical livelihood centers—including artisanal gold miners in Abu Shendi (`31+` killed) and Jabal al-Uqaydat (`72+` killed), border traders in Amiet Market (`80+` killed), and livestock shoppers in Nyala (`102+` killed).
- **Critical Energy & Health Infrastructure Collapse**:
  - **Petroleum Transit**: Precision drone strikes against Crude Oil Pumping Station 4 in [[Haiya]] damaged South Sudanese crude transit pumps and severed the municipal power line, plunging Haiya into blackouts.
  - **Hospital Closures**: The knockout of Um Darfa Hospital in Blue Nile State and the devastation of healthcare facilities in El Obeid (only ~30% functional, Maternity and Kuwaiti hospitals damaged) have deprived hundreds of thousands of emergency surgical and maternity care.
- **Starvation as a Method of Warfare & Custodial Torture**: In both [[El Fasher]] and [[El Obeid]], tight military blockades by the RSF have intentionally obstructed humanitarian convoys and commercial food trucks, while SAF Security Cells in El Obeid pack over 2,000 detainees in conditions breeding cholera and torture.

## 4. Political Economy, Regional Proxy Logistics & Diplomatic Warfare

- **Emirati Logistics Pipeline**: Investigative reports and civil society submissions confirm that the RSF’s sustained drone and artillery capabilities are underwritten by an illicit supply network financed and orchestrated by the [[United Arab Emirates]] via Amdjarass Airfield in Chad and suspected facilities in [[Assosa]], Ethiopia ([[2026-10-30 The Guardian: Sudanese Diaspora and Rights Groups Denounce UAE Nominee for UN Top Job]]).
- **Cross-Border Staging Allegations**: On 31 October 2026, Sudan Foreign Ministry Undersecretary [[Muawiya Osman]] formally alleged that the drones striking Haiya were launched from Ethiopian territory with Emirati support, highlighting regional proxy tensions between Port Sudan, Addis Ababa, and Abu Dhabi.
- **Multilateral Integrity & Geneva Negotiations**: In [[Geneva]], Swiss State Secretary [[Alexandre Fasel]] and UN Personal Envoy [[Pekka Haavisto]] convened the Consultative Meeting on Peace Initiatives on 21 October 2026 to harmonize mediation tracks and demand an aerial de-escalation, while international human rights groups mobilized against diplomatic whitewashing.
