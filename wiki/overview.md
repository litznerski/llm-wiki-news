## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Theatre Map

By **October 2026**, the war between the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) and the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) entered an acute phase of long-range unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV / drone) attrition ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]], [[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]). According to UN High Commissioner for Human Rights [[Volker Türk]] ([[OHCHR]]), drone attacks now account for the **majority of conflict-related civilian deaths in Sudan in 2026**, killing **at least 306 people** and injuring hundreds more during the first three weeks of October 2026 alone (`2026-10-01` to `2026-10-23`) ([[2026-10-23 Volker Türk Condemnation of Sudan Drone Escalation and Arms Influx]]).

Both belligerents have systematically deployed strike drones against civilian congregation hubs, IDP shelters, commercial markets, and medical infrastructure across [[West Kordofan]] (`SD18`), [[Blue Nile State]] (`SD17`), and the disputed, demilitarized border area of [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`). The deadliest single incidents in October 2026 included the **18 October "double-tap" repeat drone strike** on a transportation truck in [[Rapid Support Forces]]-controlled **Dibab** ([[West Kordofan]]) that killed **at least 139 people** ([[2026-10-18 Dibab Double-Tap Drone Strike]]), and the **19 October drone strike** on **Amiet Market** and **Todach** in demilitarized [[Abyei]] that killed **more than 80 people** and injured **more than 27 others** evacuated by [[UNISFA]] peacekeepers ([[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]). On **31 October 2026**, [[United Nations]] Secretary-General [[António Guterres]]—speaking through Spokesperson [[Stéphane Dujarric]] ([[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]])—formally condemned the indiscriminate drone attacks across Sudan and [[Abyei]] as violations of international humanitarian law and urged an immediate cessation of hostilities ([[2026-10-31 Stéphane Dujarric UNSG Statement Condemning Escalating Drone Attacks in Sudan and Abyei]], [[2026-10-31 António Guterres Appeal for Immediate Cessation of Hostilities and Good-Faith Mediation in Sudan]]).

## 3. Humanitarian Crisis, Displacement & Infrastructure Telemetry

- **Internal Displacement & Attacks on IDP Shelters**: [[OHCHR]] spokesperson [[Seif Magango]] confirmed that displaced populations are repeatedly targeted in their places of refuge—with **at least 25 civilians killed** in alleged [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] drone strikes on IDP shelters in Ghubeish and El Nuhud ([[West Kordofan]], 6–10 Oct) and **at least 31 displaced civilians killed** (mostly women and children) in an alleged [[Rapid Support Forces]] drone strike while mining gold near a displacement site in Abu Shendi ([[Blue Nile State]], 9 Oct) ([[2026-10-23 Seif Magango Statement on Evisceration of Civilian Infrastructure in Sudan]]).
- **Healthcare, Market & Utility Evisceration**:
  - **Medical Collapse**: On 11 October 2026, an [[Rapid Support Forces]] drone strike rendered **Um Darfa hospital** in [[Blue Nile State]] non-operational and seriously injured two health workers.
  - **Market Destruction in [[Abyei]]**: On 19 October 2026, drone strikes burned down **Amiet Market** in [[Abyei]], where [[UNISFA]] Pakistani battalion commander Lt. Col. [[Muhammad Farooq]] reported *"every shop was burning"* ([[2026-10-23 Muhammad Farooq First-Responder Account of Amiet Market Fire]]) and survivor [[Angelina Musa Diaw]] testified: *"No work, everything is gone. I am here with my children. But I need help"* ([[2026-10-23 Angelina Musa Diaw Survivor Testimony on Amiet Market Destruction]]).
  - **Peacekeeper Trauma Response**: [[UNISFA]] Acting Force Commander Brig. Gen. [[Adebowale Ajabge]] confirmed the evacuation of **more than 27 injured civilians** to the [[UNISFA]] hospital in Todach ([[2026-10-23 Adebowale Ajabge Report on UNISFA Emergency Response and Todach Evacuations]]).

## 4. Political Economy, Regional Diplomacy & External Sponsorship

- **War Economy, Arms Influx & UAV Proliferation**: UN High Commissioner [[Volker Türk]] warned on 23 October 2026 that the continuous external influx of arms and drones is fueling systematic violations of international humanitarian law ([[2026-10-23 Volker Türk Condemnation of Sudan Drone Escalation and Arms Influx]], [[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]).
- **UN Security Council & UN Secretary-General IHL Determinations**: Following the [[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]], the UN Security Council condemned the attack as a potential **war crime**, demanded an immediate investigation, and reaffirmed that [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`) must remain demilitarized and free of all forces except [[UNISFA]]. On **31 October 2026**, [[United Nations]] Secretary-General [[António Guterres]] (via Spokesperson [[Stéphane Dujarric]], [[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]]) issued a formal communiqué (`A1`) condemning the indiscriminate UAV strikes as violations of international humanitarian law and calling for an immediate cessation of hostilities and good-faith mediation toward an inclusive political settlement ([[2026-10-31 Stéphane Dujarric UNSG Statement Condemning Escalating Drone Attacks in Sudan and Abyei]], [[2026-10-31 António Guterres Appeal for Immediate Cessation of Hostilities and Good-Faith Mediation in Sudan]]).
