## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Theatre Map

By **early November 2026**, the war between the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) and the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) entered an acute phase of asymmetric long-range unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV / drone) warfare, deep-rear infrastructure interdiction, and heightened multilateral accountability scrutiny ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]], [[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]). According to UN High Commissioner for Human Rights [[Volker Türk]] ([[OHCHR]]) and the UN Fact-Finding Mission, drone attacks have become the **leading cause of conflict-related civilian deaths in Sudan in 2026**, with verified fatalities exceeding **510 civilians killed** during October 2026 alone.

The strategic landscape is marked by seven major developments:
1. **Systemic Deep-Rear Power Grid Interdiction**: Following the 30 October drone strike disabling the primary power substation in [[Shendi]] ([[2026-10-30 Shendi Power Substation Drone Strike]]) and the 31 October strike on Station 4 in [[Haiya]] ([[2026-10-31 Haiya Oil Pumping and Power Station Drone Strike]]), RSF long-range drones struck key electricity transformer stations in [[Abu Hamad]] ([[River Nile State]]) and [[Al Dabbah]] ([[Northern State]]) on 1 November ([[2026-11-01 Abu Hamad and Al Dabbah Power Stations Drone Strikes]]), part of a calculated campaign incapacitating 10–14 power stations across 9 states. Concurrently in [[White Nile State]], the destruction of the main transmission grid plunged [[Ad Douiem]] and Kosti into rolling crises with ice blocks surging to 30,000 SDG and commercial water station collapse ([[2026-10-26 White Nile Power Grid Blackout and Economic Crisis]]).
2. **Urban Market Carnage & Forensic Triangulation in Darfur**: The 27 October airstrike on Tanqonj livestock market in [[Nyala]] ([[South Darfur]]) resulted in **102 verified civilian fatalities** and over 188 injuries through a calculated double-tap delivery ([[2026-10-27 Nyala Tanqonj Market Drone Strike]]), with [[Médecins Sans Frontières]] receiving 36 dead at the field hospital while >70 bodies were buried directly in collective mass graves in Doumaya.
3. **Formal UNSC Diplomatic Escalation Against Cross-Border Drone Staging**: On 1 November 2026, the Sudan Ministry of Foreign Affairs formally lodged complaints with the UN Security Council and African Union Peace and Security Council invoking UN Charter Article 2(4) against [[Government of Ethiopia]], alleging that long-range UAVs targeting Haiya, Abu Hamad, and Merowe are supplied by the [[United Arab Emirates]] and launched directly from Assosa Airbase ([[2026-11-01 Sudan UNSC and AU Complaint Against Ethiopia]]).
4. **RSF Governance Centralization & Dissolution of Civil Bodies**: RSF Commander [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]] issued Decree No. 19 of 2026 on 31 October dissolving all regional civil administrations in Darfur and West Kordofan, stripping native tribal administrations of executive authority and centralizing administrative power under [[Tasees]] secretariats ([[2026-10-31 RSF Dissolution of Native Administration Document]]).
5. **Commercial Convoy Interdiction & Acute Famine Triggers**: RSF drone strikes hit commercial trade trucks in [[El Malha]] ([[North Darfur]]) on 1 November ([[2026-11-01 El Malha Commercial Vehicle Drone Strike]]), compounding severe drought and agricultural failure across northern Darfur. In East [[Jebel Marra]], local leadership in [[Dar Omo]] and [[Liba]] warned of imminent mass starvation due to a 10-month total commercial blockade, locust swarms, and healthcare collapse ([[2026-10-30 Dar Omo Famine and Humanitarian Crisis Alert]]). In [[River Nile State]], the [[National Umma Party]] declared an acute famine emergency due to agricultural irrigation failure and wheat production collapse.
6. **Critical Healthcare Grid Failure**: The national cancer treatment facility, [[Khartoum Oncology Hospital]], suffered a catastrophic power and fuel disruption halting linear accelerators and cobalt therapy for 300+ daily cancer patients ([[2026-11-01 Ahmed Omar Appeal for Khartoum Oncology Radiotherapy]]).
7. **Civil & Labour Resistance**: Defying wartime repression, the [[Sudanese Teachers Committee]] launched a general strike demanding payment of 18 months of withheld wartime salaries ([[2026-11-01 Sudanese Teachers Committee Strike Communique]]), while a coalition of 8 feminist and legal organizations issued a landmark declaration condemning indiscriminate drone and artillery warfare as international war crimes ([[2026-10-30 Sudanese Feminist Coalition Condemnation of Drone Warfare]]).

## 3. Humanitarian Crisis, Displacement & Infrastructure Telemetry

- **Systemic Targeting of Economic Congregation Points & Convoys**: Conflict actors have systematically expanded strikes to civilian commercial lifelines—including livestock shoppers in Nyala (`102+` killed), border traders in Amiet Market (`80+` killed), artisanal miners in Abu Shendi (`31+` killed) and Jabal al-Uqaydat (`72+` killed), and commercial supply trucks in El Malha (3 food trucks destroyed).
- **Critical Energy Grid Paralysis & Healthcare Collapse**:
  - **Power Transmission Sabotage**: Precision drone strikes against Crude Oil Pumping Station 4 in [[Haiya]], the municipal substation in [[Shendi]], and transformer stations in [[Abu Hamad]] and [[Al Dabbah]], alongside grid collapse in [[White Nile State]], have paralyzed municipal water, hospital refrigeration, and agricultural irrigation schemes across Northern, River Nile, and White Nile states.
  - **Oncology & Emergency Healthcare Halt**: At [[Khartoum Oncology Hospital]], blackouts severed power to radiotherapy linear accelerators and cooling chillers, halting treatment for 300+ daily cancer patients and endangering refrigerated radioactive isotopes. In [[Liba]] (East Jebel Marra), the sole health center has been non-operational for 10 months, driving catastrophic maternal and infant mortality. In El Obeid, only ~30% of health facilities remain functional.
- **Famine Escalation & Cross-Border Displacement**:
  - **Darfur Desert Drought & Convoy Interdiction**: Complete failure of the summer agricultural season in [[El Malha]] coupled with commercial route interdiction has pushed the locality into IPC Phase 4/5 conditions. Over 45,000 newly displaced people from El Fasher and surrounding villages have congregated in [[Karnoi]] and [[Um Baru]] along the Chadian frontier without shelter or food.
  - **East Jebel Marra Famine Alert**: In [[Dar Omo]] and [[Liba]], over 12,000 displaced persons face catastrophic IPC Phase 5 starvation due to locust infestations destroying the sesame and millet crops ("white autumn") and commercial blockades preventing market entry.
  - **River Nile Famine Warning**: The [[National Umma Party]] issued an emergency famine alert for River Nile State due to soaring fuel costs, power grid destruction, and the collapse of the winter wheat crop cycle.

## 4. Political Economy, Regional Proxy Logistics & Diplomatic Warfare

- **Cross-Border Staging & Legal Escalation Against Ethiopia**: Following precision strikes on Haiya, Abu Hamad, and Al Dabbah, Sudan formally submitted an Article 2(4) complaint to the UN Security Council alleging that the [[Government of Ethiopia]] allows the UAE to operate weaponized drone supply flights and staging launchpads from Assosa Airbase in Benishangul-Gumuz.
- **RSF Administrative Consolidation**: By enacting Decree 19 of 2026, Gen. [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]] dissolved all regional civil administrations in RSF-held zones, dismantling traditional native authority structures in favor of centralized [[Tasees]] leadership, provoking resentment among traditional Darfuri tribal elders.
- **International Condemnation of Drone Escalation**: The [[Embassy of France in Sudan]] and the [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Egypt)]] issued strong public condemnations of the nationwide drone attacks on civilian markets and power infrastructure, while the [[Sudanese Communist Party]] warned that drone warfare has become an existential weapon threatening state cohesion.
- **Feminist & Civil Society Mobilization Against Drone Warfare**: Eight Sudanese civil society and legal groups led by [[No to Women's Oppression Initiative]], [[Darfur Bar Association]], and [[Al-Jazira Center for Human Rights]] issued an urgent joint declaration on 30 October classifying the widespread use of combat UAVs and heavy artillery as war crimes, appealing directly to the UN Security Council for an enforceable nationwide arms embargo.
- **Expert & Think Tank Opposition to UAE UNSG Candidacy**: Former UN Security Council sanctions investigator [[Dino Mahtani]] (in [[Middle East Eye]]) and the [[Centre for International Policy]] documented that the UAE UN Mission under [[Lana Nusseibeh]] systematically dismissed verified sanctions-busting, subjected UN personnel to retaliatory transit detention, and coordinated with Bahrain to stall Security Council reports on Colombian mercenaries in [[El Fasher]], while leveraging $1.4T in US investment frameworks and European AI deals to insulate its candidate from P5 vetoes.
- **Civil Labour Resistance**: The [[Sudanese Teachers Committee]] spearheaded a nationwide civil strike against the war economy, decrying the allocation of national resources to military hardware while hundreds of thousands of public servants remain unpaid.
