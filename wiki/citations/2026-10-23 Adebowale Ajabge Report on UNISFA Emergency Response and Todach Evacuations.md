## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

Brigadier General [[Adebowale Ajabge]] serves as **Acting Force Commander** of the **United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei** ([[UNISFA]]), holding operational command over UN peacekeeping forces in the demilitarized [[Abyei]] area (`SD18001`). Following the **19 October 2026** drone attack on Amiet Market and Todach that killed more than 80 people ([[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]), Brig. Gen. [[Adebowale Ajabge]] detailed [[UNISFA]]'s emergency stabilization, firefighting, and casualty evacuation operations ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).
