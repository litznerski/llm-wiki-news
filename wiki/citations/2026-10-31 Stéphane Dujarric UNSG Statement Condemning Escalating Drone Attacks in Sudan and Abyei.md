## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On **31 October 2026**, [[Stéphane Dujarric]], Spokesperson for [[United Nations]] Secretary-General [[António Guterres]], issued a formal executive statement via the [[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]] addressing the nationwide escalation of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV / drone) attacks across Sudan and the disputed [[Abyei]] area (`SD18001`) ([[sources/2026-10-31-unsg-spokesperson-escalating-drone-attacks-sudan]]). Delivered eight days after [[OHCHR]] High Commissioner [[Volker Türk]] and spokesperson [[Seif Magango]] documented `306+` civilian deaths from drone strikes across [[West Kordofan]], [[Blue Nile State]], and [[Abyei]] ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]), this communiqué elevated the legal condemnation to the highest executive office of the [[United Nations]].

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Executive Communiqué (English Original — `2026-10-31`)
> "The Secretary-General strongly condemns the escalating drone attacks in Sudan by the parties to the conflict and the catastrophic toll these attacks are inflicting on civilians. Across the country, as well as in Abyei, drones have killed hundreds of civilians, including women and children, which constitute violations of international humanitarian law.
>
> The Secretary-General is appalled by all the attacks directed against civilians and critical infrastructure, including health facilities."
> — **[[Stéphane Dujarric]]**, Spokesman for Secretary-General **[[António Guterres]]** ([[United Nations]], [[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]], `2026-10-31`) ([[sources/2026-10-31-unsg-spokesperson-escalating-drone-attacks-sudan]])
