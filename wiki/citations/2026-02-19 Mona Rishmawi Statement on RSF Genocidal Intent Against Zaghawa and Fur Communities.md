## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 19 February 2026, international jurist [[Mona Rishmawi]] delivered the UN Fact-Finding Mission's legal evaluation establishing that genocidal intent was the only reasonable inference from the totality of evidence gathered on RSF conduct in [[El Fasher]] (`SD02001`).

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-02-19`)
> "The body of evidence we collected – including the prolonged siege, starvation and denial of humanitarian assistance, followed by mass killings, rape, torture and enforced disappearance, systematic humiliation and perpetrators’ own declarations – leaves only one reasonable inference. The RSF acted with intent to destroy, in whole or in part, the Zaghawa and Fur communities in El Fasher. These are the hallmarks of genocide."
> — **[[Mona Rishmawi]]**, Expert Member of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (`2026-02-19`)
