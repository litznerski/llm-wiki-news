## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 1 November 2026, the Sudanese [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Sudan)]] officially deposited formal complaint instruments with both the UN Security Council in New York and the African Union Peace and Security Council in Addis Ababa, charging the [[Government of Ethiopia]] with acts of cross-border aggression.

> 
> **English Forensic Translation:**
> "The Government of Sudan lodged a formal complaint against Ethiopia with both the UN Security Council and the African Union Peace and Security Council, alleging neighboring Ethiopia's complicity in attacks on civilian sites, objects, and electricity stations in several Sudanese states... The Government of Sudan appended all documents and evidence confirming the Rapid Support Forces' use of Ethiopian territory to launch suicide and missile-equipped combat drones targeting civilian objects and vital installations... This constitutes an act of aggression placing Ethiopia before international law, holding it accountable for violating Article 2(4) of the UN Charter."
