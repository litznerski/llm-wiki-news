## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 19 February 2026, [[Mohamed Chande Othman]], Chair of the UN Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan, formally released the Mission's landmark investigative report establishing that crimes committed during and after the RSF takeover of [[El Fasher]] in late October 2025 bore the defining characteristics and hallmarks of genocide.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-02-19`)
> "The scale, coordination, and public endorsement of the operation by senior RSF leadership demonstrate that the crimes committed in and around El Fasher were not random excesses of war. They formed part of a planned and organized operation that bears the defining characteristics of genocide. Perpetrators at all levels of authority must be held accountable. Where evidence indicates genocide, the international community has a heightened obligation to prevent, protect and ensure justice is done."
> — **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]**, Chair of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (`2026-02-19`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Planned Operation under Senior Command**: Refutes RSF claims of undisciplined rogue elements; demonstrates that the atrocities were centrally coordinated.
- **Legal Threshold of Genocide**: Triggers heightened legal obligations under the 1948 Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide.
