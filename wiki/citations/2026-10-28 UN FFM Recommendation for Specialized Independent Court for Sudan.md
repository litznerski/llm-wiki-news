# 2026-10-28 UN FFM Recommendation for Specialized Independent Court for Sudan

## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

> [!NOTE] Speaker Provenance & Policy Architecture
> In the operative recommendations of Conference Room Paper `A/HRC/65/CRP.1` released on 28 October 2026, the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** formally recommended that the international community establish a specialized independent judicial mechanism—in the form of a dedicated tribunal or hybrid court for the Sudan—operating in close tandem with the **[[International Criminal Court]]** (ICC). Chaired by former Tanzanian Chief Justice **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]**, the Mission formulated this structural policy recommendation to address entrenched impunity and bridge gaps between international jurisdiction and comprehensive domestic transitional justice.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Recommendation for Specialized Independent Court (A/HRC/65/CRP.1, Section IV, Para 95(d))
> **English Verbatim:**
> > "Address impunity, ensure accountability and provide justice to victims including through a specialized independent judicial mechanism in the form of a specialized court for the Sudan working in tandem with the International Criminal Court, alongside a comprehensive transitional justice system, strengthened rule of law and due process guarantees in domestic legal processes. Co-operate with the International Criminal Court, support documentation processes, and ensure access to Sudan by regional and international accountability mechanisms."
>
> **Arabic Translation / Terminology Reference:**
> > "معالجة ظاهرة الإفلات من العقاب، وضمان المساءلة، وتحقيق العدالة للضحايا، بما في ذلك من خلال آلية قضائية مستقلة متخصصة تتمثل في إنشاء محكمة متخصصة للسودان تعمل بالتنسيق مع المحكمة الجنائية الدولية، إلى جانب نظام شامل للعدالة الانتقالية، وتعزيز سيادة القانون وضمانات الإجراءات القانونية الواجبة في الإجراءات القضائية المحلية. والتعاون مع المحكمة الجنائية الدولية، ودعم عمليات التوثيق، وضمان وصول آليات المساءلة الإقليمية والدولية إلى السودان."

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Institutional Architecture for Accountability**: Proposes a dual-track accountability architecture combining ICC universal jurisdiction with a specialized hybrid court capable of prosecuting mid-level commanders, auxiliary paramilitaries, and war economy financiers.
- **Evidence Preservation Conduit**: Links the FFM’s repository of digital, testimonial, and satellite evidence directly to criminal admissibility standards, ensuring seamless handover to international prosecutors.
- **Counter to Belligerent Immunity Claims**: Formally rejects transitional power-sharing agreements that grant blanket amnesties to senior military or militia commanders implicated in war crimes and crimes against humanity.
