## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

[[Hamdan Suleiman]] is a civilian resident of the Doumaya neighborhood in [[Nyala]] whose 19-year-old son was killed in the 27 October 2026 double-tap drone strike on the Tanqonj marketplace. He provided immediate testimony to Agence France-Presse and [[BBC News]] Arabic via WhatsApp.
