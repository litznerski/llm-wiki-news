## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On **23 October 2026**, [[Volker Türk]], acting in his official capacity as **United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights** at the head of [[OHCHR]], issued a formal condemnation from Geneva addressing the surge in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) strikes across Sudan between 1 and 23 October 2026 ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]]). His statement accompanied [[OHCHR]] spokesperson [[Seif Magango]]'s briefing confirming that at least 306 people had been killed by drone strikes in three weeks across [[West Kordofan]], [[Blue Nile State]], [[Abyei]], Darfur, and Khartoum State ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> "The increasing use of drones, already accounting for the majority of conflict-related civilian deaths in Sudan this year, is deeply disturbing and is exacting a horrifying toll."
>
> "Broader peace efforts must squarely address the continued influx of arms that is fuelling the conflict and press for an end to the transfer of arms that are used in manifest disregard of the IHL obligations protecting civilians. I urge both parties to the conflict, and their affiliates, to commit to a ceasefire."
> — **[[Volker Türk]]**, UN High Commissioner for Human Rights ([[OHCHR]]), 23 October 2026 ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]])
