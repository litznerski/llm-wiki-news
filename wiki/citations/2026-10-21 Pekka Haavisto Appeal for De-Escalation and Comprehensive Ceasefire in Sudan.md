## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 21 October 2026, [[Pekka Haavisto]], Personal Envoy of UN Secretary-General António Guterres for Sudan, opened the de-escalation session of the Geneva consultative peace talks, appealing to international delegations to mobilize their bilateral leverage to secure an immediate ceasefire.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-21`)
> "Our first session, devoted to de-escalation, will offer an opportunity to explore practical ways forward... [Delegates should identify measures and use their] respective channels of engagement [to] build confidence and help create conditions conducive to a comprehensive ceasefire... Our role is to support the Sudanese in shaping their country’s shared future, with due regard for the diversity of their voices and experiences."
> — **[[Pekka Haavisto]]**, Personal Envoy of the Secretary-General for Sudan, **[[United Nations]]** (`2026-10-21`)
