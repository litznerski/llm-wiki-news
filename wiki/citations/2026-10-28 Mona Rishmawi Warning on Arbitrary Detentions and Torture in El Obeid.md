## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, international jurist and UN Fact-Finding Mission expert member [[Mona Rishmawi]] issued an urgent warning regarding the severe contraction of civic space, arbitrary arrests, and systemic torture documented in [[El Obeid]] (`SD13001`), carried out by the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] and military intelligence.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "The people of El Obeid live in constant fear not only of the RSF drones, but also of the militarisation of their city and the campaigns of arbitrary detention as reprisals for perceived disloyalty to the SAF or civic engagement, including supporting human rights and peace efforts. Thousands have been arrested by the SAF’s security cells and detained and tortured in unofficial locations as well as in the El Obeid Central Prison under appalling conditions. These practices must stop immediately and independent bodies that monitor detention must be granted access."
> — **[[Mona Rishmawi]]**, Expert Member of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (`2026-10-28`)

---

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **SAF Security Cells & Torture**: Establishes that thousands of civilians, lawyers, and aid workers have been arbitrarily detained and tortured by SAF security cells in El Obeid.
- **Demand for Independent Access**: Calls for immediate cessation of secret detention practices and unhindered access for international detention monitors.
