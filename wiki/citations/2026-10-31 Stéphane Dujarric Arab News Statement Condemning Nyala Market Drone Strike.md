## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 31 October 2026, UN Spokesperson [[Stéphane Dujarric]] briefed wire journalists following a catastrophic SAF drone strike on Monday, 27 October 2026, which struck Tanqonj Market in [[Nyala]] (`SD03001`), South Darfur, killing at least 102 people.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-31`)
> "The secretary-general strongly condemns the escalating drone attacks in Sudan by the parties to the conflict and the catastrophic toll these attacks are inflicting on civilians... Drones have killed hundreds of people across the country and constitute violations of international humanitarian law. The secretary-general calls on the parties to spare the civilian population and critical infrastructure and agree without delay to a cessation of hostilities as a first step toward ending this devastating conflict."
