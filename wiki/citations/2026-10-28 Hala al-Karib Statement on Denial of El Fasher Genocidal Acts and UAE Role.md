## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, leading Sudanese women's rights and pro-democracy advocate [[Hala al-Karib]], Regional Director of the SIHA Network, addressed the global civil society briefing opposing the UAE's nomination of [[Lana Nusseibeh]] for UN Secretary-General, asserting that endorsing the candidate would constitute a denial of the genocidal violence in [[El Fasher]].

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Symbolic Erasure of Genocide**: Argues that elevating an Emirati diplomat who dismissed evidence of UAE weapon shipments represents an erasure of justice for Fur and Zaghawa victims in El Fasher.
- **Accountability for Gender-Based Violence**: Demands that external sponsors of militias executing systematic sexual violence in Darfur be held accountable rather than rewarded with UN leadership.
