## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 21 October 2026, Swiss State Secretary [[Alexandre Fasel]], co-hosting the high-level peace consultations with the United Nations in [[Geneva]], issued a strong indictment of the shift toward long-range unmanned aerial vehicle warfare in Sudan, linking the "drone war" directly to civilian starvation.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-21`)
> "The war in Sudan has become a drone war; it kills mostly civilians. Famine-like conditions persist in Darfur, the Kordofan state and elsewhere in the country; it is the war that drives this hunger. The Geneva Conventions are very clear: the protection of civilians and humanitarian access must not wait for a ceasefire and a political solution. Civilians are suffering and dying today."
> — **[[Alexandre Fasel]]**, State Secretary of Switzerland, **[[Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (Switzerland)]]** (`2026-10-21`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Characterization as a "Drone War"**: Formally codifies the diplomatic recognition that long-range UAVs have become the dominant weapon system driving conflict fatalities.
- **Geneva Conventions Mandate**: Asserts that humanitarian obligations under international law are absolute and cannot be deferred pending political settlements.
