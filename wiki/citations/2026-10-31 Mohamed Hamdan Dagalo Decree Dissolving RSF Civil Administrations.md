## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 31 October 2026, [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]] issued Presidential Decree No. (19) of 2026 from [[Nyala]], officially revoking the decentralized 2024 Civil Administrations Document and abolishing all civilian councils across five RSF-controlled states.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement
> **Arabic Original:**
> "القرار يأتي في إطار الانتقال التأسيسي التدريجي، بغية تنظيم مؤسسات الدولة وإعادة هيكلتها، وضمان انتظام العمل التنفيذي والإداري إلى حين استكمال الأطر التشريعية المنظمة للحكم اللامركزي والمحلي... إلغاء وثيقة الإدارات المدنية لسنة 2024 وحل الإدارات المدنية والمجالس التأسيسية في كل من: جنوب دارفور، ووسط دارفور، وشرق دارفور، وغرب دارفور، وغرب كردفان."
> 
> **English Forensic Translation:**
> "This decision comes within the framework of the gradual founding transition, aimed at organizing and restructuring state institutions and guaranteeing the regularity of executive and administrative work until the completion of legislative frameworks governing decentralized and local governance... Revoking the 2024 Civil Administrations Document and dissolving the civil administrations and founding councils in each of: South Darfur, Central Darfur, East Darfur, West Darfur, and West Kordofan."
> — **[[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]]**, President of the Presidential Council of **[[Tasees]]** (quoted in **[[Radio Dabanga]]**, `2026-11-02`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Institutional Abolition**: Confirms the wholesale dissolution of earlier decentralized councils, documenting the RSF’s transition to centralized ministerial secretariats.
- **Geographic Scope**: Demonstrates that paramilitary administrative control spans four Darfur states and West Kordofan.
