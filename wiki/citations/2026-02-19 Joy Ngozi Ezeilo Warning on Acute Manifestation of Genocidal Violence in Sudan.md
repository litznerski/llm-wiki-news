## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 19 February 2026, Professor [[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]], expert member of the UN Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan, warned that the genocidal violence documented in [[El Fasher]] was not an isolated episode but an acute manifestation of structural patterns of ethnic destruction rapidly expanding into other regions of Sudan.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-02-19`)
> "The conduct in El Fasher was an acute manifestation of patterns consistent with genocidal violence. With the conflict expanding to other regions, including Kordofan, urgent civilian protection is needed now more than ever."

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Spillover of Atrocity Patterns**: Warns that the methods used in Darfur (starvation sieges, ethnic targeting, and drone strikes) are being replicated across Kordofan and eastern Sudan.
- **Urgent Civilian Protection Mandate**: Demands immediate multilateral intervention and civilian protection mechanisms to prevent further mass killings.
