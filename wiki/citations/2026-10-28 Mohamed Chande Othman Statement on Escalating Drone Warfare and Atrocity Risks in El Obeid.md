## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, former Chief Justice of Tanzania and Chair of the UN Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan, [[Mohamed Chande Othman]], presented the findings of the Mission's comprehensive inquiry into the human rights and humanitarian catastrophe in [[El Obeid]] (`SD13001`). He issued a stark warning regarding the rapid technological escalation and geographic expansion of drone warfare.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "The situation in and around El-Obeid remains grave and the risk of atrocities are high. The attacks reported in Blue Nile, Abyei and near the Sudan-Egypt border reinforce what we have been warning about for months: the conflict is becoming more geographically extensive, more technologically sophisticated and increasingly dangerous for civilians. Our findings from El Obeid show that civilians remain under attack, while restrictions on fundamental freedoms make life very difficult."
> — **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]**, Chair of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (`2026-10-28`)
