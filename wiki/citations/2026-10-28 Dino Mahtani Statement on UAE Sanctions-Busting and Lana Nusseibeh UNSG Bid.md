## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, [[Dino Mahtani]], who previously served as an official United Nations Security Council Sanctions Panel investigator documenting illicit trade and armed group financing, authored a comprehensive forensic dispatch in [[Middle East Eye]]. Mahtani intervened during the global diplomatic mobilization surrounding the nomination of Emirati diplomat [[Lana Nusseibeh]] to succeed António Guterres as [[United Nations]] Secretary-General.

Drawing on his first-hand operational interactions with the UAE Mission to the UN during Nusseibeh's ambassadorship, Mahtani detailed how her office routinely dismissed direct photographic and documentary evidence of sanctions evasion (including Somali charcoal shipments funding al-Shabaab) and described his subsequent detention and expulsion by UAE state security in 2022.
