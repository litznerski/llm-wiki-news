## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, [[Mohamed Chande Othman]], Chair of the UN Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan, addressed the Third Committee (Social, Humanitarian and Cultural Issues) of the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, presenting the Mission's comprehensive findings on war crimes, drone proliferation, and systemic human rights abuses.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Address Excerpt (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "The armed conflict in the Sudan has generated one of the gravest humanitarian and human rights catastrophes of our time. Across Darfur, Kordofan, Khartoum and eastern Sudan, civilians are subjected to deliberate, indiscriminate, and disproportionate attacks by both the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces. The rapid influx of advanced weaponry, including unmanned aerial vehicles, has drastically exacerbated civilian suffering. The international community has an inescapable obligation to act decisively: to enforce the arms embargo, halt illicit supply pipelines, and ensure that those responsible for atrocity crimes are held to account before international justice."
> — **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]**, Chair of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (`2026-10-28`)
