## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, Senior Advocate of Nigeria and UN Fact-Finding Mission expert member Professor [[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]] highlighted the compounding, long-term humanitarian reverberations of attacks on critical civilian infrastructure documented in [[El Obeid]] and across Sudan.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "One year after the devastation of El Fasher, civilians in other parts of Sudan continue to pay the price of a conflict that is becoming increasingly destructive and difficult to escape. In El Obeid, attacks on electricity networks, water facilities, hospitals, schools and displacement sites demonstrate how damage to critical civilian infrastructure can reverberate through entire communities long after an attack has occurred."
> — **[[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]]**, Expert Member of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (`2026-10-28`)
