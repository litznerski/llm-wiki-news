## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

Lieutenant Colonel [[Muhammad Farooq]] commands the Pakistani peacekeeping battalion deployed with the **United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei** ([[UNISFA]]) inside the demilitarized border area of [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`). Following the **19 October 2026** drone attack on **Amiet Market** and **Todach** ([[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]), Lt. Col. [[Muhammad Farooq]] led the first peacekeeping units to reach the bombed market site ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).
