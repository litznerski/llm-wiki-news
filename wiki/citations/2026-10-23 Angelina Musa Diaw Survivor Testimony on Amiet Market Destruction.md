## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

[[Angelina Musa Diaw]] is a civilian market trader and mother of three children at **Amiet Market** in the demilitarized border area of [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`). Following the **19 October 2026** drone attack that killed more than 80 people and burned the market to the ground ([[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]), she provided direct survivor testimony to UN personnel documenting the total loss of household livelihoods ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]]).
