# 2026-10-28 UN FFM Finding on SAF Detention Practices and Torture in El Obeid

## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

> [!NOTE] Speaker Provenance & Legal Mandate
> In Section IV of Conference Room Paper `A/HRC/65/CRP.1` presented to the [[UN Human Rights Council]] on 28 October 2026, the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** formally established that detention practices enforced by the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) and affiliated Security Cell units in [[El Obeid]] violate fundamental international human rights law (IHRL) and international humanitarian law (IHL), qualifying these acts as war crimes—specifically cruel treatment, torture, and outrages upon personal dignity under the Rome Statute.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Forensic Finding on SAF Custodial War Crimes (A/HRC/65/CRP.1, Section IV, Para 89)
> **English Verbatim:**
> > "The mission finds that the detention practices applied by the Sudanese Armed Forces in El Obeid amount to serious violations of international humanitarian law, including violence to life and person and outrages upon dignity, as well as violations of international human rights law, including the prohibition of torture and other cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment, and the right to life, liberty, security of person, health and adequate food. Such conduct may amount to war crimes, particularly cruel treatment, torture, and outrages upon personal dignity."
>
> **Arabic Translation / Terminology Reference:**
> > "تخلص البعثة إلى أن ممارسات الاحتجاز التي تطبقها القوات المسلحة السودانية في الأبيض ترقى إلى انتهاكات جسيمة للقانون الدولي الإنساني، بما في ذلك الاعتداء على الحياة والسلامة البدنية والاعتداء على الكرامة الشخصية، فضلاً عن انتهاكات القانون الدولي لحقوق الإنسان، بما في ذلك حظر التعذيب وغيره من ضروب المعاملة أو العقوبة القاسية أو اللاإنسانية أو المهينة. وقد ترقى هذه الأفعال إلى مصاف جرائم الحرب، ولا سيما المعاملة القاسية، والتعذيب، والاعتداء على الكرامة الشخصية."

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Institutional Attribution to SAF Command**: Formally pierces the veil of deniability surrounding the El Obeid joint "Security Cell" (*Al-Khaliyya al-Amniyya*), holding SAF military command legally accountable for abuses inside military facilities and El Obeid Central Prison.
- **Specific Torture Modalities Documented**: Connects the legal qualification directly to empirical findings: beatings with hoses and whips, prolonged incommunicado detention, extortion, denial of medical care resulting in cholera deaths, and sexual coercion against female detainees.
- **Debunking Emergency Law Protections**: The finding explicitly rules that neither armed conflict nor state-of-emergency declarations under national law permit the suspension of non-derogable prohibitions against torture and arbitrary killing.
