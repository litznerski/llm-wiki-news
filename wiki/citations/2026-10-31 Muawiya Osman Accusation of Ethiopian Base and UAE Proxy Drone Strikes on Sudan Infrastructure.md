## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 31 October 2026, Ambassador [[Muawiya Osman]], Undersecretary of the Sudanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs, delivered a formal briefing in Port Sudan to foreign diplomatic missions accredited to Sudan. The briefing was convened following repeat drone strikes on energy and oil pumping infrastructure in [[Haiya]] (`SD10002`).

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (Bilingual Forensic Text — `2026-10-31`)
> **Arabic Original:**
> "هذه الهجمات بالطائرات المسيرة التي تصاعدت وتيرتها خلال الأشهر الأخيرة استهدفت البنيات التحتية والمنشآت الحيوية بالبلاد، لا سيما مرافق إنتاج الكهرباء ومحطات المياه والمدارس والمستشفيات ومحطات الوقود، وأسفرت عن استشهاد وإصابة المئات... قاعدة أصوصا الإثيوبية أصبحت مركزاً لإمداد ما اسماه بالمليشيا المتمردة بالأسلحة والعتاد، وتسهيل عبور المقاتلين الأجانب والمرتزقة المشاركين في الحرب على السودان، مؤكداً أن الأخطر يتمثل في استخدام القواعد والأراضي الإثيوبية منطلقاً لشن هجمات بالطائرات المسيّرة على البلاد."
> 
> **English Forensic Translation:**
> "These drone attacks, which have escalated in frequency in recent months, targeted infrastructure and vital facilities in the country—particularly electricity production facilities, water stations, schools, hospitals, and fuel stations—resulting in the martyrdom and injury of hundreds... The Ethiopian Assosa base has become a hub for supplying the rebel militia with weapons and equipment, and facilitating the transit of foreign fighters and mercenaries participating in the war on Sudan, emphasizing that the most dangerous aspect is the use of Ethiopian bases and territory as a launchpad to conduct drone attacks against the country."
> — Ambassador **[[Muawiya Osman]]**, Undersecretary of the **[[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Sudan)]]** (`2026-10-31`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Direct Attribution to Assosa Base**: Formally accuses Ethiopia of allowing the [[Assosa]] base to serve as an operational staging ground for RSF drone strikes on eastern Sudan.
- **Accusation of UAE Proxy Finance**: Asserts that regional sponsors (principally the [[United Arab Emirates]]) finance and direct these cross-border weapons transfers and mercenary corridors.
