# 2026-10-28 UN FFM Finding on RSF Drone Warfare and Indiscriminate Attacks in El Obeid

## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

> [!NOTE] Speaker Provenance & Mandate Authority
> On 28 October 2026, the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (FFM), chaired by former Chief Justice of Tanzania **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]** alongside expert jurists **[[Mona Rishmawi]]** and **[[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]]**, presented Conference Room Paper `A/HRC/65/CRP.1` to the 65th session of the [[UN Human Rights Council]]. Mandated under HRC Resolution 62/1 to conduct an urgent inquiry into atrocities in and around [[El Obeid]], the Mission delivered a landmark legal finding establishing that the [[Rapid Support Forces]]' (RSF) campaign of long-range drone strikes in populated urban areas violates core international humanitarian law (IHL) rules of distinction, proportionality, and precautions, amounting to war crimes.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Forensic Finding on RSF Drone Warfare (A/HRC/65/CRP.1, Section IV, Para 84)
> **English Verbatim:**
> > "The mission has reasonable grounds to believe that the Rapid Support Forces conducted drone strikes, in populated areas of El Obeid, resulting in multiple deaths and injuries, as documented in section II.C above. Repeated strikes affected residential neighbourhoods, educational facilities, locations where civilians had gathered or sought shelter, and civilian infrastructure, including fuel stations and electricity infrastructure. The manner in which drone strikes are taking place violates the obligations of the Rapid Support Forces to respect the principles of distinction, proportionality and precautions in attack. Such violations may amount to war crimes."
>
> **Arabic Translation / Terminology Reference:**
> > "تخلص البعثة إلى وجود أسباب معقولة للاعتقاد بأن قوات الدعم السريع شنت هجمات بالطائرات المسيّرة على مناطق مأهولة بالسكان في الأبيض، مما أسفر عن سقوط قتلى وجرحى في صفوف المدنيين. إن الطريقة التي تُشن بها هذه الضربات تنتهك التزامات قوات الدعم السريع باحترام مبادئ التمييز والتناسب واتخاذ الاحتياطات في الهجوم، وقد ترقى هذه الانتهاكات إلى مصاف جرائم الحرب."

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Judicial Standard of Proof Met**: Establishes "reasonable grounds to believe" based on 29 direct witness testimonies, satellite damage verification, and munitions analysis under the Berkeley Protocol.
- **Systematic Targeting of Civilian Objects**: Directly references documented strikes on Shikan educational warehouses (destroying 330,000 learning materials), University of Kordofan dormitories (5 dead, 47 wounded), and the Al Mina Al Bari displacement camp.
- **Legal Rebuttal to Belligerent Defenses**: Debunks RSF claims that drone strikes targeted only SAF military objectives, establishing that the foreseeable blast and fragmentation radius in densely populated neighborhoods violated the principle of proportionality.
