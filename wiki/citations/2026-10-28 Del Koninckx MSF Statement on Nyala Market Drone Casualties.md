## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, [[Del Koninckx]], Project Coordinator for [[Médecins Sans Frontières]] at Nyala Teaching Hospital, issued a critical operational debrief and condemnation following a devastating SAF drone strike on the crowded Tanqonj / Dumaya marketplace in [[Nyala]] (`SD03001`), South Darfur.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "Why are markets being targeted? This is horrific. Markets, homes, and essential civilian infrastructure are not targets, and all parties to the conflict must protect civilians. Medical staff worked around the clock to treat patients, including women and children, who were deeply traumatised. Wounded were transported to hospital in tuk-tuks and donkey-drawn carts."
> — **[[Del Koninckx]]**, MSF Project Coordinator in Nyala (quoted in **[[Radio Dabanga]]**, `2026-10-28`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Hospital Admissions Verification**: Confirms that Nyala Teaching Hospital admitted 36 deceased individuals (with 21 specifically verified in civilian attire) and treated over 150 injured patients bearing blast fragmentation and burn trauma.
- **IHL Violation Grounding**: Establishes that the targeted location was a civilian marketplace where populations gathered for basic nourishment, challenging military framing that depicted the target as a military installation.
