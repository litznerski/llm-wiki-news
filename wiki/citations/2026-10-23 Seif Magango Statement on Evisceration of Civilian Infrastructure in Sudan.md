## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On **23 October 2026**, [[Seif Magango]], speaking as **Spokesperson for the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights** ([[OHCHR]]), presented the office's verified field findings on the three-week wave of drone strikes across Sudan (`2026-10-01` to `2026-10-23`). His briefing detailed the deaths of at least 306 civilians and the systematic destruction of IDP shelters, markets, hospitals, power stations, and water facilities across [[West Kordofan]], [[Blue Nile State]], and [[Abyei]] ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]]).

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> "Scores of civilians have been killed, including internally displaced persons, and vital infrastructure and services have been eviscerated, driving further displacement and deepening the already severe humanitarian crisis."
> — **[[Seif Magango]]**, Spokesperson for [[OHCHR]], 23 October 2026 ([[sources/2026-10-23-un-news-sudan-drone-strikes-306-killed]])
