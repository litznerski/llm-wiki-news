## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "I have taken a leave of absence from my ministerial post in the UAE government to be mindful of the obligations of independence the UN role would entail. The crimes committed by the RSF are atrocious, and the horrific conflict in Sudan will be the litmus test of the success of an incoming secretary general. If appointed, I will support the work of UN fact-finding missions for accountability and transitional justice over the war in Sudan."
