## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, [[Kenneth Roth]], long-time executive director of Human Rights Watch, addressed a virtual civil society briefing organized by Avaaz, challenging the United Arab Emirates' nomination of diplomat [[Lana Nusseibeh]] for United Nations Secretary-General over the UAE's material role in fueling mass atrocities in Sudan.

---

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "It’s fair to say the RSF would not have been able to be nearly as successful militarily as it was and has been without that support from the UAE. And with that support, it didn’t just fight the Sudan armed forces. This wasn’t just a civil war. It committed mass atrocities. She has been asked about the UAE’s role in arming and supporting the RSF, and she just blatantly denies it without regard to the facts. That is not what we need as UN secretary-general. It amazes me that she’s being put forward. This is a completely unsuitable candidate."
> — **[[Kenneth Roth]]**, Former Executive Director, **[[Human Rights Watch]]** (`2026-10-28`)

## 3. Substantive Claims, Event Grounding & Contradiction Impact

- **Decisive Impact of UAE Arms**: Establishes that the RSF's military capacity and ability to perpetrate mass atrocities in Darfur directly depend upon UAE weapon and logistics pipelines.
- **Disqualification on Independence Grounds**: Argues that Nusseibeh's public denials of documented arms transfers disqualify her from serving as an independent guardian of the UN Charter.
