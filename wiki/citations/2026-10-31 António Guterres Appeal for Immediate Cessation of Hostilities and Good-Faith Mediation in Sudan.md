## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On **31 October 2026**, [[United Nations]] Secretary-General [[António Guterres]]—speaking through Spokesperson [[Stéphane Dujarric]] and the [[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]]—issued an urgent diplomatic appeal to the warring parties in Sudan ([[Sudanese Armed Forces]] and [[Rapid Support Forces]]) following three weeks of catastrophic unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) strikes across Sudan and [[Abyei]] ([[sources/2026-10-31-unsg-spokesperson-escalating-drone-attacks-sudan]], [[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]]).

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Executive Appeal (English Original — `2026-10-31`)
> "The Secretary-General calls on the parties to spare the civilian population and critical infrastructure and agree without delay to a cessation of hostilities as a first step towards ending this devastating conflict. He renews his urgent appeal to the parties to engage in good faith with mediation efforts.
>
> The United Nations stands ready to support any efforts to advance a sustainable political resolution that reflects the aspirations of the Sudanese people."
> — **[[António Guterres]]**, Secretary-General of the **[[United Nations]]** (delivered by **[[Stéphane Dujarric]]**, [[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]], `2026-10-31`) ([[sources/2026-10-31-unsg-spokesperson-escalating-drone-attacks-sudan]])
