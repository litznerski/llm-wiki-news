## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 1 November 2026, the Higher Strike Committee of the [[Sudanese Teachers Committee]] announced the continuation of its rolling strike across [[Khartoum State]] to demand wage increases and the settlement of salary arrears.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement
> **Arabic Original:**
> "المشاركة الفاعلة في الحراك والإضراب واجب على كل معلم ومعلمة لحماية مستقبل الأجيال وكرامة المهنة، داعية الجميع للمشاركة الصلبة والالتزام بجدول الإضراب للأسبوعين القادمين في ولاية الخرطوم... للمطالبة برفع الحد الأدنى للأجور وسداد كافة المتأخرات، والتزام الدولة بالإنفاق على التعليم."
> 
> **English Forensic Translation:**
> "Active participation in this mobilization and strike is a duty upon every female and male teacher to protect the future of generations and the dignity of the profession, urging everyone to participate steadfastly and adhere to the strike schedule for the next two weeks in Khartoum State... demanding an increase in the minimum wage, payment of all arrears, and state commitment to expenditure on education."
> — **[[Sudanese Teachers Committee]]** Higher Strike Committee statement (quoted in **[[Radio Dabanga]]**, `2026-11-02`)
