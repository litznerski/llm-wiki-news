## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, the [[Embassy of France in Sudan]] released a formal diplomatic declaration condemning the acute escalation of airstrikes and combat drone attacks executed by conflict parties across Sudan.

## 2. Verbatim Bilingual Citation & Forensic Text

> [!QUOTE] Verbatim Statement (English Original — `2026-10-28`)
> "The recent escalation in air strikes was entirely unacceptable, wherever they occurred, including Darfur, Khartoum, Abyei, the northern Nile Valley, Kordofan, and Blue Nile. The attacks had killed hundreds of civilians, including many children, within a few days. There is no military solution to the conflict in Sudan."
