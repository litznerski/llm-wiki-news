## 1. Speaker Provenance, Institutional Role & Context

On 28 October 2026, the Washington-based [[Centre for International Policy]] (CIP), an independent research organization promoting human rights-centered and diplomacy-first foreign policy, released an official institutional statement regarding the selection of the next [[United Nations]] Secretary-General.

The statement intervened amidst growing international scrutiny regarding the candidacy of Emirati diplomat [[Lana Nusseibeh]], warning the international community against elevating an official inextricably linked to the autocratic governance and proxy warfare apparatus of the [[United Arab Emirates]].
