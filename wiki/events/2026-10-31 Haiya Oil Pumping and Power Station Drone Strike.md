# 2026-10-31 Haiya Oil Pumping and Power Station Drone Strike

> [!NOTE] 5W1H Event Anchor
> - **What**: Long-range strike drone attack targeting an electrical transformer substation under construction and the main crude oil pipeline pumping station, setting crude oil storage tanks on fire.
> - **Who**: Perpetrated by the [[Rapid Support Forces]] against SAF-controlled energy infrastructure.
> - **Where**: [[Haiya]] (`SD10002`), [[Red Sea State]] (`SD10`), eastern Sudan.
> - **When**: Saturday, 31 October 2026 (second strike within 72 hours).
> - **Why**: Disruption of state energy transmission and crude oil export logistics.

---

## 1. Event Anchor & 5W1H Summary

On 31 October 2026, the [[Rapid Support Forces]] carried out a repeat long-range strike drone attack against strategic energy facilities in [[Haiya]] (`SD10002`), [[Red Sea State]]. The attack struck both the newly constructed electrical power transformer substation and the vital crude oil pumping station along the pipeline carrying crude oil to coastal export terminals at Bashayer. The strike ignited multiple crude oil storage tanks, generating massive fires and compounding widespread electrical outages across eastern Sudan.

---

## 2. Chronological Sequence & Tactical Mechanics ("What & How")

- **28 October 2026**: Initial RSF drone strike hits electrical transmission facilities in Haiya.
- **31 October 2026 (Morning)**: A second wave of strike drones penetrates eastern airspace, directly impacting oil pumping machinery and fuel reservoirs.
- **Immediate Response**: Civil defense teams mobilized to contain fires at the crude storage facility; transmission lines disconnected across the regional grid.

---

## 3. Causal Drivers & Strategic Motives ("Why")

- **Economic Strangulation**: Paralyze the sole operational crude oil export corridor sustaining Sudan and South Sudan.
- **Asymmetric Deep Strike**: Demonstrate RSF capability to strike deep into government-held eastern strongholds proximate to [[Port Sudan]].

---

## 4. Humanitarian, Territorial & Geopolitical Impact

- **Power Outages**: Aggravated nationwide grid collapse, with over 13 power stations suffering drone damage across seven states.
- **Diplomatic Escalation**: Prompted the Sudanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs to convene an urgent diplomatic briefing accusing Ethiopia and the UAE of launching UAVs from the [[Assosa]] base.
