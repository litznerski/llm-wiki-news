# 2026-10-28 Global Civil Society Briefing Opposing Lana Nusseibeh Candidacy for UN Secretary-General

> [!NOTE] 5W1H Event Anchor
> - **What**: High-level virtual press briefing convened by international civil society and Sudanese diaspora groups to oppose the United Arab Emirates' nomination of Lana Nusseibeh for UN Secretary-General.
> - **Who**: Sudanese advocates ([[Hala al-Karib]], [[Ibrahim Alduma]], [[Husam Mahjoub]]) and international rights figures ([[Kenneth Roth]], [[Bruno Stagno Ugarte]], [[Kate Ferguson]]).
> - **Where**: Virtual briefing organized from New York / London.
> - **When**: Tuesday, 28 October 2026.
> - **Why**: Protest UAE covert military and weapons backing for the Rapid Support Forces and prevent what advocates describe as "autocratic capture" of the United Nations.

---

## 1. Event Anchor & 5W1H Summary

On 28 October 2026, members of the Sudanese diaspora, Sudanese women's rights networks, and international human rights monitors organized an urgent global briefing hosted by Avaaz to denounce the candidature of Emirati diplomat [[Lana Nusseibeh]] to succeed António Guterres as [[United Nations]] Secretary-General. Speakers presented extensive evidence documenting the UAE's role as the primary foreign sponsor of the [[Rapid Support Forces]], arguing that appointing a senior Emirati diplomat would whitewash mass atrocities committed in El Fasher and compromise the institutional integrity of the UN.

---

## 2. Chronological Sequence & Tactical Mechanics ("What & How")

- **Mid-October 2026**: The UAE formally nominates Lana Nusseibeh for UN Secretary-General following an inconclusive informal Security Council straw poll.
- **28 October 2026**: Global briefing convenes featuring former HRW executive director [[Kenneth Roth]], SIHA Regional Director [[Hala al-Karib]], and Sudanese community leaders.
- **28 October 2026**: Former UN Security Council Sanctions Panel investigator [[Dino Mahtani]] publishes an exposé in [[Middle East Eye]], revealing how the UAE UN Mission under Nusseibeh systematically dismissed verified sanctions-busting evidence and detailing his own 2022 retaliatory detention by UAE authorities ([[2026-10-28 Dino Mahtani Statement on UAE Sanctions-Busting and Lana Nusseibeh UNSG Bid]]).
- **28 October 2026**: The [[Centre for International Policy]] issues an institutional statement condemning the nomination, warning that Nusseibeh's career owes itself to an authoritarian regime serving as a global kleptocracy hub ([[2026-10-28 Centre for International Policy Statement on Lana Nusseibeh UN Candidacy]]).
- **28–30 October 2026**: Widespread international media coverage (led by The Guardian and [[Middle East Eye]]) sparks diplomatic debate among UN Security Council permanent members, revealing that a UN panel report on UAE-deployed Colombian mercenaries in [[El Fasher]] had been stalled by Bahrain.

---

## 3. Causal Drivers & Strategic Motives ("Why")

- **Evidentiary Linkage**: The briefing aimed to directly tie the UN Secretary-General selection to ground realities in Sudan, specifically the UN findings of "hallmarks of genocide" in El Fasher fueled by UAE-supplied weaponry and mercenaries.
- **Preserving Multilateral Independence**: Prevent a state accused of violating the UN arms embargo and orchestrating cyber influence operations against UN experts (as established by Anthropic in September 2026) from assuming the executive leadership of the global body.

## 4. Humanitarian, Territorial & Geopolitical Impact

- **Elevated Sudan War Profile**: Forced international attention on the external proxy dynamics driving the war in Sudan.
- **Diplomatic Deadlock**: Complicated Security Council straw polls, increasing scrutiny on UAE foreign policy among veto-wielding permanent members.
