# 2026-11-01 Sudan UNSC and AU Complaint Against Ethiopia

> [!NOTE] 5W1H Event Anchor
> - **What**: Formal sovereign legal complaints submitted to the UN Security Council and the African Union Peace and Security Council accusing Ethiopia of facilitating RSF drone warfare.
> - **Who**: Lodged by the [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Sudan)]]; accusing the [[Government of Ethiopia]] of aiding the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF).
> - **Where**: Port Sudan / New York (UNSC) / Addis Ababa (AU-PSC); referencing the [[Assosa]] airbase in Ethiopia.
> - **When**: Sunday, 1 November 2026.
> - **Why**: International legal mobilization to hold Ethiopia accountable under Article 2(4) of the UN Charter for facilitating drone strikes on 14 Sudanese power substations.

---

## 1. Event Anchor & 5W1H Summary

On Sunday, 1 November 2026, the Sudanese **[[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Sudan)]]** formally submitted bilateral complaints to the **United Nations Security Council** and the **African Union Peace and Security Council**, directly charging the **[[Government of Ethiopia]]** with complicity in hostile cross-border military aggression against Sudan. The legal filings invoked Article 2, Paragraph 4 of the UN Charter, asserting that Ethiopia violated international law by permitting the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) to utilize its territory—specifically the **[[Assosa]]** military airfield—as a launch site and logistics conduit for suicide and missile-armed combat drones.

The complaint included comprehensive evidentiary dossiers, radar telemetry, and weapons debris documentation linking cross-border drone incursions to the destruction of **14 critical electricity distribution stations** across nine Sudanese states (Gedaref, Khartoum, Gezira, Red Sea, Sennar, White Nile, South Kordofan, River Nile, and Northern State). The filing demanded that the Security Council and the African Union enforce punitive international legal measures against Addis Ababa and designate the RSF a terrorist militia.

## 2. Chronological Sequence & Tactical Mechanics ("What & How")

- **31 October 2026**: Undersecretary Ambassador [[Muawiya Osman]] convenes accredited foreign diplomats in Port Sudan, previewing intelligence findings on the Assosa drone pipeline.
- **1 November 2026**: Sudanese permanent missions in New York and Addis Ababa officially deposit comprehensive complaint dossiers with the UNSC Presidency and the AU-PSC Secretariat.
- **Evidentiary Dossier Components**: The documentation includes UAV flight vectors, transponder intercepts, satellite imagery of staging ramps in Benishangul-Gumuz, and forensic missile components recovered from targeted power substations.
- **2 November 2026**: The Sudanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs issues a public communique summarizing the legal complaint.

## 3. Causal Drivers & Strategic Motives ("Why")

- **International Isolation of RSF Supply Networks**: By officially taking Ethiopia before the UN Security Council, Khartoum seeks to dismantle the transnational supply corridors linking the UAE to the RSF via Ethiopian and Chadian airbases.
- **Diplomatic Pre-emption**: Escalates pressure on Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed ahead of AU summits, raising the diplomatic cost of Ethiopian intelligence facilitation for the RSF.

## 4. Humanitarian, Territorial & Geopolitical Impact

- **Severe Regional Rift**: Pushes Sudan-Ethiopia bilateral relations to their lowest point since the Al-Fashaga border clashes of 2020–2022.
- **Multilateral Standoff**: Puts the AU Peace and Security Council in the delicate position of mediating a direct interstate dispute between two key member states.
