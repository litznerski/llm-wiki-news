# 2025-10 El Fasher Siege and RSF Takeover Atrocities

> [!NOTE] 5W1H Event Anchor
> - **What**: Culmination of an 18-month (500-day) starvation siege resulting in the military capture of El Fasher, accompanied by ethnically targeted mass killings, widespread sexual violence, and forced expulsion exhibiting the **hallmarks of genocide**.
> - **Who**: Perpetrated by the [[Rapid Support Forces]] and allied Arab militias against non-Arab (Zaghawa and Fur) civilian populations and defeated SAF/Joint Force defenders.
> - **Where**: [[El Fasher]] (`SD02001`), capital of [[North Darfur]] (`SD02`).
> - **When**: Late October 2025 (formally documented in UN reports February 2026).
> - **Why**: Complete elimination of non-Arab resistance and consolidation of total RSF hegemony over the Darfur region.

---

## 1. Event Anchor & 5W1H Summary

In late October 2025, the [[Rapid Support Forces]] breached and captured [[El Fasher]], the final state-held capital in Darfur, following a 500-day siege that deliberately subjected 1.5 million civilians to starvation, water deprivation, and continuous shelling. According to the [[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]], the subsequent takeover was marked by planned, systematic mass atrocities against non-Arab communities (primarily Zaghawa and Fur) that satisfied statutory criteria for genocide under international law.

---

## 2. Chronological Sequence & Tactical Mechanics ("What & How")

- **May 2024 – Oct 2025**: Total encirclement of El Fasher, blocking all humanitarian convoys, bombing hospitals, and shelling [[Zamzam IDP Camp]].
- **20–25 October 2025**: Multi-axis ground assault supported by heavy artillery and loitering munitions collapses the SAF 6th Division defensive perimeter.
- **26–31 October 2025**: Post-capture ethnic slaughter; fighters conduct house-to-house searches, executing non-Arab males, committing systematic sexual violence against Fur and Zaghawa females, and expelling survivors into the desert.

---

## 3. Causal Drivers & Strategic Motives ("Why")

- **Genocidal Intent**: Forensically established by survivor testimonies recording explicit perpetrator incitement to eliminate non-Arab populations (*"We want to eliminate anything black from Darfur"*).
- **Territorial Domination**: Final elimination of state and rebel movement presence across all five Darfur states.

---

## 4. Humanitarian, Territorial & Geopolitical Impact

- **Total Hegemony in Darfur**: Solidified RSF control over western Sudan and established parallel administrative control based in [[Nyala]].
- **Mass Displacement & Famine**: Forced over 500,000 survivors into mountainous enclaves and Chad; expanded IPC Phase 5 Famine conditions.
