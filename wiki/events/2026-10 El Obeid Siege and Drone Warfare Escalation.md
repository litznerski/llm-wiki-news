# 2026-10 El Obeid Siege and Drone Warfare Escalation

> [!NOTE] 5W1H Event Anchor
> - **What**: Partial encirclement, recurring strike drone attacks on civilian infrastructure, and severe state surveillance and arbitrary detention campaigns.
> - **Who**: [[Rapid Support Forces]] (perpetrating UAV strikes on civilian sites), [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (co-locating troops in residential areas and executing mass detentions), and [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]] (quartered in residential sectors).
> - **Where**: [[El Obeid]] (`SD02001`), capital of [[North Kordofan]] (`SD02`).
> - **When**: October 2026 (`2026-10-01` to `2026-10-28`), following preparatory escalations in June–September 2026.
> - **Why**: Strategic struggle for the primary logistical pivot between Khartoum and western Sudan.

---

## 1. Event Anchor & 5W1H Summary

Throughout October 2026, the strategic capital of [[North Kordofan]], [[El Obeid]], experienced an acute escalation of dual-front violence documented extensively by the [[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]] in Conference Room Paper `A/HRC/65/CRP.1`. The city, home to over half a million residents and over 100,000 internally displaced persons, remained subjected to a campaign of long-range loitering munition attacks by the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF), whose strike drones repeatedly hit residential neighborhoods, educational facilities, displacement shelters, and municipal water and power networks. Concurrently, the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) 5th Infantry Division and affiliated joint Security Cells (*Al-Khaliyya al-Amniyya*) militarized civilian neighborhoods—housing combatants from the [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]] in civilian apartments—and instituted pervasive arbitrary arrests, incommunicado detentions, and custodial torture in El Obeid Central Prison.

---

## 2. Chronological Sequence & Tactical Mechanics ("What & How")

- **June 2026 Preparatory Onset**: RSF drone strikes intensify, striking fuel stations, the SAF 5th Division HQ, and municipal power stations. Successive strikes hit Al-Muwazzafeen, Al-Amir, and Al-Matar neighborhoods. On 10–11 June, strikes hit homes in Al-Muwazzafeen near billets of the [[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]], killing 3+ civilians. On 16 June, a strike damages the maternity hospital's operating rooms and pharmacy.
- **Late July 2026 Corridor Reopening**: SAF and allied units recapture Bara and Jabrat al-Sheikh, breaking the physical encirclement and reopening the northern highway to Khartoum.
- **September 2026 Infrastructure & Educational Strikes**:
  - **9 September**: Drone strike damages the College of Developmental and Technological Sciences, killing a security guard.
  - **20 September**: Precision drone strikes hit 2 warehouses in Shikan locality, destroying 330,000 learning materials.
  - **28 September**: Strike in the Sheikan College and Sports City area wounds multiple civilians.
  - **30 September**: Strike on the Al Mina Al Bari displacement site kills 1 child and wounds 12 others.
- **October 2026 Climax & Custodial Terror**:
  - **1 October**: Drone strike on a University of Kordofan dormitory kills 5 civilians and injures 47.
  - **Mid to Late October**: SAF Security Cells intensify arrests of trade unionists, teachers, and emergency room responders; El Obeid Central Prison population surges past 2,000 in a 450-capacity facility, precipitating lethal cholera outbreaks.

---

## 3. Causal Drivers & Strategic Motives ("Why")

- **RSF Strategic Harassment**: Paralyze the SAF garrison and degrade civilian municipal resilience to force the surrender of the vital transit gateway to Darfur.
- **SAF Security Paranoia & Social Control**: Weaponize emergency laws to suppress civil society monitoring, eliminate alleged fifth-column informants, and prevent independent documentation of military co-location in civilian quarters.

---

## 4. Humanitarian, Territorial & Geopolitical Impact

- **Healthcare Decimation**: Repeated strikes on electricity, fuel, and medical facilities reduced operational capacity so that only about 30% of hospitals remained functional in El Obeid.
- **Sub-Human Custodial Conditions**: In Security Cell detention rooms, 35 to 40 detainees were packed into 2.5×2m cells; over 200 detainees shared single toilets, with confirmed cholera deaths in police custody.
- **Disproportionate Impact on Women**: Water station disruptions forced women and girls to collect water at night, exposing them to sexual violence, while female detainees faced sexual harassment and extortion by Security Cell personnel.
