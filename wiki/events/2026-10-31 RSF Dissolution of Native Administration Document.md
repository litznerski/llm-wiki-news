# 2026-10-31 RSF Dissolution of Native Administration Document

> [!NOTE] 5W1H Event Anchor
> - **What**: Promulgation of Presidential Decree No. (19) of 2026 revoking the 2024 Civil Administrations Document and dissolving all state civil administrations and founding councils across RSF-controlled regions.
> - **Who**: Issued by [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]] ("Hemedti"), Commander of the [[Rapid Support Forces]] and President of the Presidential Council of [[Tasees]].
> - **Where**: [[Nyala]] (`SD03001`), South Darfur; affecting South Darfur, Central Darfur, East Darfur, West Darfur, and West Kordofan.
> - **When**: Friday, 31 October 2026.
> - **Why**: Transitioning paramilitary civil governance from decentralized tribal councils toward centralized executive secretariats under direct RSF high command.

---

## 1. Event Anchor & 5W1H Summary

On Friday, 31 October 2026, [[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]] ("Hemedti"), acting in his capacity as President of the Presidential Council of [[Tasees]] and Commander of the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF), issued Presidential Decree No. (19) of 2026. The decree formally abolished the 2024 Civil Administrations Document and dissolved all civil administrations and founding councils established across RSF-controlled territory in South Darfur, Central Darfur, East Darfur, West Darfur, and West Kordofan.

The decree relieved all appointed civilian governors, council presidents, and executive advisors, ordering the reorganization of state governance under transitional administrative secretariats directly answerable to Tasees headquarters in [[Nyala]]. Immediately following the proclamation, regional administrative bodies rebranded their official portals to reflect the new structure (such as the "Secretariat of the Government of South Darfur State").

---

## 2. Chronological Sequence & Tactical Mechanics ("What & How")

- **31 October 2026**: Hemedti signs Presidential Decree No. (19) of 2026 in Nyala, invoking powers as President of the Presidential Council of Tasees.
- **Immediate Executive Orders**: The decree dissolves all state civil administrations, founding councils, sub-units, and consultative bodies in five states.
- **Administrative Transition**: Caretaker executive secretariats are tasked with maintaining municipal utilities, market taxation, and public order pending permanent local governance laws.
- **1 November 2026**: South Darfur Civil Administration renames its public facing portals to "Secretariat of the Government of South Darfur State (Tasees)."

---

## 3. Causal Drivers & Strategic Motives ("Why")

- **Re-Centralization of Authority**: The decentralized civil administration system created in early 2024 generated severe tribal friction, elite infighting, and localized corruption among Darfuri traditional leaders, undermining RSF central command.
- **Professionalization & Statecraft Mimicry**: Hemedti sought to replace informal tribal-based councils with bureaucratic "secretariats" to project institutional professionalism to international observers and aid agencies.

---

## 4. Humanitarian, Territorial & Geopolitical Impact

- **Consolidation of Paramilitary Rule**: Solidifies the RSF's institutional grip over Darfur and West Kordofan, reinforcing the territorial bifurcation of Sudan into an RSF-administered west and an SAF-administered north and east.
- **Administrative Uncertainty**: Raised acute concern among humanitarian agencies regarding the legal status of operating permits, travel authorizations, and local tax exemptions negotiated under the dissolved 2024 document.
