## 1. Source Metadata & 5W1H Provenance Anchor

- **Who**: Perpetrated by the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF); reported by [[Radio Dabanga]]; confirmed by the [[Shendi Locality Security Committee]].
- **What**: Suicide drone strike targeting the Shendi electricity transformer substation, causing severe fire, destroying key transformers, and cutting municipal power completely. Additional drone strikes targeted an air defense facility, a reserve fuel storage depot of the [[Nile Petroleum Company]], and the SAF [[Third Infantry Division]] headquarters.
- **Where**: [[Shendi]] (`SD08004`), [[River Nile State]], Sudan.
- **When**: Dawn of Friday, 30 October 2026.
- **Why**: Deep-strike attrition campaign by the RSF targeting utility infrastructure and military command installations in government-held riverine rear states, expanding across seven states.
