## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that the Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs (**[[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Egypt)]]**) released a formal diplomatic communique strongly condemning the escalating drone strikes against civilian and vital infrastructure in Sudan. Characterizing the attacks as blatant violations of international humanitarian law, Egypt demanded immediate adherence to civilian protection standards.

Critically, the Egyptian foreign ministry underscored the urgency of halting the illicit influx of weapons flowing across neighboring countries into the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF). Reaffirming its commitment to Sudan’s unity, territorial integrity, and sovereign institutions, Cairo warned regional states against meddling in internal Sudanese affairs and urged immediate ceasefire mediation.
