## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 30 October 2026, **[[The Guardian]]** reported that members of the Sudanese diaspora, grassroots activist organizations, and leading international human rights monitors mounted a vigorous campaign denouncing the candidacy of Emirati diplomat **[[Lana Nusseibeh]]** for the post of **[[United Nations]]** Secretary-General to succeed António Guterres. 

The campaign centers on the **[[United Arab Emirates]]**' well-documented covert military, logistical, and financial support for the **[[Rapid Support Forces]]** (RSF). Critics cite Nusseibeh's record as UAE ambassador to the UN (until 2024), where she repeatedly offered blanket denials of UN Panel of Experts and Fact-Finding Mission reports detailing UAE arms shipments to the RSF. At a virtual civil society briefing organized by Avaaz on 28 October 2026, Sudanese activist [[Hala al-Karib]] (Regional Director of the [[SIHA Network]]), [[Ibrahim Alduma]] (Co-Founder of Sudan Action Hub), [[Husam Mahjoub]] (Co-Founder of Sudan Bukra TV), former Human Rights Watch executive director [[Kenneth Roth]], and [[Kate Ferguson]] of Protection Approaches argued that appointing an Emirati senior diplomat would amount to "autocratic capture" of the UN and a whitewashing of genocidal atrocities committed in El Fasher.

In response, Nusseibeh stated she had taken a leave of absence from her UAE ministerial post to guarantee independence, pledged to support UN fact-finding missions on Sudan, and described RSF crimes as "atrocious," asserting that the Sudan conflict would be the litmus test of the incoming Secretary-General.
