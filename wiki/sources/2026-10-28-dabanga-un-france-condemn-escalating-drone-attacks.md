## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 28 October 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that both the **[[Embassy of France in Sudan]]** and the **[[UN OCHA]]** issued stern condemnations regarding the nationwide surge in aerial drone attacks across Sudan. The French Embassy warned that escalating airstrikes across Darfur, Khartoum, Abyei, the northern Nile Valley, Kordofan, and Blue Nile had killed hundreds of civilians, including numerous children, within mere days, reaffirming that "there is no military solution to the conflict in Sudan."

Simultaneously, UN OCHA highlighted that systematic drone strikes against critical energy infrastructure—including a major electricity substation serving Khartoum on 27 October, and earlier strikes in El Gezira, Sennar, and El Gedaref on 26 October—severely threatened the collapse of the national power grid, cutting off essential water and health utilities for millions of non-combatants.
