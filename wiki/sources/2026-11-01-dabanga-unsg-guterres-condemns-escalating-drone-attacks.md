## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, independent broadcaster **[[Radio Dabanga]]** published an Arabic news dispatch reporting on the statement attributable to the Spokesperson for United Nations Secretary-General **[[António Guterres]]** regarding the catastrophic escalation of unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) attacks across Sudan and **[[Abyei]]**. 

The article provides the authoritative Arabic translation of the Secretary-General's condemnation, recording that drone strikes by both warring parties have killed hundreds of civilians—including women and children—in actions constituting direct violations of international humanitarian law. Radio Dabanga highlighted Guterres's call on both belligerents to spare civilians and critical infrastructure, to agree without delay to a cessation of hostilities, and to engage in good faith with mediation efforts.

Critically, Radio Dabanga contextualized the Secretary-General's statement with the findings released earlier that week by the UN Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan, which warned of expanding drone warfare, severe repression, arbitrary detention, and credible allegations of sexual violence committed by the Sudanese Armed Forces in El Obeid, as well as RSF drone attacks threatening civilian settlements.
