## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported from [[Nyala]] that the President of the Presidential Council of "[[Tasees]]" and Commander of the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF), Lieutenant General **[[Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo]]** ("Hemedti"), promulgated Presidential Decree No. (19) of 2026 on Friday, 31 October 2026. The decree formally revokes the 2024 Civil Administrations Document and dissolves all state civil administrations and founding councils established across RSF-controlled regions in South Darfur, Central Darfur, East Darfur, West Darfur, and West Kordofan.

The decree relieved all appointed heads of civil administrations, council presidents, executive officers, and ministerial advisors. In his decree, Hemedti stated that the measure was part of a "gradual founding transition" aimed at restructuring state institutions and maintaining executive regularity pending the completion of legislative frameworks for decentralized governance. Following the promulgation, the Civil Administration of South Darfur rebranded its official government channels as the "Secretariat of the Government of South Darfur State (Tasees)."
