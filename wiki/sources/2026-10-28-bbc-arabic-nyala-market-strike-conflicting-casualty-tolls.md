## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 28 October 2026, [[BBC News]] Arabic published an in-depth investigative dispatch detailing the contested casualty tolls and operational mechanics of the aerial drone strike on a crowded marketplace in [[Nyala]] (`SD03001`), the capital of [[South Darfur]] (`SD03`) and administrative headquarters of the [[Rapid Support Forces]]-allied "Tasees" administration. The dispatch documented direct eyewitness testimonies establishing a **double-tap drone strike doctrine**: a primary strike hit the market around 7:30 AM, followed 10 minutes later by a second strike on the identical location as civilians, vendor neighbors, and emergency responders converged to recover casualties.

BBC Arabic highlighted sharp discrepancies across initial casualty figures: the legal advocacy network [[Emergency Lawyers]] verified 102 civilian fatalities and 113 injuries, while the Darfur Victims Organization initially noted 14 dead before confirming mass grave burials, and Nyala Teaching Hospital received 21 bodies. Eyewitness [[Hamdan Suleiman]] of the Doumaya district confirmed burying his 19-year-old son alongside over 70 other victims in a single collective grave. Pro-SAF military sources disputed civilian targeting, asserting the airstrikes targeted a paramilitary training camp and the 16th Infantry Division headquarters. Additionally, the dispatch reported broader drone strikes impacting the [[Haiya]] oil pumping station and power substations in El Managil, as well as severe child acute malnutrition alerts in Jebel Marra.
