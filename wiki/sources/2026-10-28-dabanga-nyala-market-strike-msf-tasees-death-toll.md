## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 28 October 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported critical forensic field data regarding the SAF aerial drone strike on a crowded marketplace in [[Nyala]] (`SD03001`), South Darfur. According to international medical humanitarian organization [[Médecins Sans Frontières]] (MSF), Nyala Teaching Hospital directly received **36 bodies** following the Monday evening strike on Dumaya / Tangong market, and treated more than **150 injured patients** suffering from extensive blast shrapnel wounds and severe burns. MSF project coordinator [[Del Koninckx]] reported that victims were rushed to the facility in three-wheeled tuk-tuks and donkey-drawn carts, and specifically noted that 21 of the deceased arrived wearing civilian clothes.

Simultaneously, the Health Ministry of the RSF-backed **[[Tasees]]** administration declared a catastrophic casualty figure of **188 fatalities and 356 injuries**, basing their toll on cumulative hospital admissions and community reports. The Sudan Liberation Movement led by Abdel Wahid al-Nur (**[[Sudan Liberation Movement (al-Nur)]]**) confirmed that five members of a single member's family were killed in the strike (Iman Mohammed Abulgasim, Fatima Ismail, Sara Yacoub, Hassan Abdel Moneim, and Mawahib Ali). The strike followed an earlier drone bombing on Nyala's livestock market on 16 October which killed four.
