## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that an aerial drone strike attributed to the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) struck the **Belila** (`بليلة`) area in [[Kurmuk]] (`SD08003`) locality, within the "New Funj Region" of [[Blue Nile State]] (`SD08`). The strike occurred on Saturday, 31 October 2026, impacting a bustling commercial center.

According to a formal communique issued by the **[[Sudan People's Liberation Movement-North (al-Hilu)]]** (SPLM-N), the strike killed **five civilians** and wounded four others, while completely demolishing a commercial market complex. The SPLM-N warned of an intensifying campaign of aerial drone strikes launched by government forces against inhabited population centers across the southern borderlands, where control is actively contested between the SAF, RSF, and SPLM-N forces.
