## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 19 February 2026, **[[UN News]]** journalist [[Vibhu Mishra]] reported on a landmark investigative report released by the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** concluding that the **[[Rapid Support Forces]]** (RSF) carried out ethnically targeted mass killings, widespread sexual violence, and enforced disappearances during their late-October 2025 capture of **[[El Fasher]]** (`SD02001`), the capital of **[[North Darfur]]** (`SD02`). The Mission formally determined that these acts bear the **"hallmarks of genocide"** against non-Arab communities, specifically the **Zaghawa** and **Fur** ethnic groups.

The findings establish that following an 18-month (500-day) siege that systematically starved and weakened the trapped civilian population through denial of food, water, medical supplies, and humanitarian aid, the RSF executed a planned and coordinated assault. The Mission verified at least three statutory acts of genocide under international law: killing members of the protected group, causing serious bodily and mental harm, and deliberately inflicting conditions of life calculated to bring about the group's physical destruction in whole or in part. Perpetrator declarations documented by survivors included explicit genocidal rhetoric: *"Is there anyone Zaghawa among you? If we find Zaghawa, we will kill them all"* and *"We want to eliminate anything black from Darfur."*

Mission Chair **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]**, alongside Expert Members **[[Mona Rishmawi]]** and **[[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]]**, warned that genocidal intent was the only reasonable inference from the systematic pattern of atrocities and affirmed that the international community holds a heightened obligation to prevent further mass atrocities and hold RSF commanders accountable.
