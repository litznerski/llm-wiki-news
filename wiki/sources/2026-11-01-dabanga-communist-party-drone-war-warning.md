## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] broadcast an interview with **[[Fathi Fadul]]**, official spokesperson for the **[[Sudanese Communist Party]]** (SCP). Fadul issued a grave warning regarding the structural transformation of the Sudanese conflict into an unconstrained drone war driven by advanced military technology and foreign sponsorship.

Fadul stated that combat drone strikes have shifted away from tactical military installations to deliberately target public commercial markets, hospitals, power substations, and critical civilian infrastructure, resulting in over **400 verified civilian deaths** and hundreds of injuries across Sudan. He directly analyzed the political economy of the belligerents, noting that the SAF leadership relies on backing from the Muslim Brotherhood and conservative Arab states, while the RSF receives heavy support from the [[United Arab Emirates]]. Fadul demanded decisive accountability from the UN Human Rights Council and urged the formation of a unified civilian popular front to halt the war and save over 25 million Sudanese facing acute famine and structural collapse.
