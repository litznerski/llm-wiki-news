## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 31 October 2026, independent broadcaster **[[Radio Dabanga]]** reported that the **[[Rapid Support Forces]]** (RSF) launched an unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) attack on the strategic railway and transport junction of **[[Haiya]]** (`هيا`) in **[[Red Sea State]]** (`SD10`) in eastern Sudan. The strike targeted both the Haiya electricity transformer station (currently under construction) and the critical crude oil pumping station along the export pipeline, setting multiple crude oil storage tanks ablaze. This constituted the second drone strike targeting energy and power facilities in Haiya within a 72-hour window. Radio Dabanga noted that over 13 electricity distribution stations across seven states (Gedaref, Khartoum, Al Jazirah, Red Sea, Sennar, White Nile, and South Kordofan) have suffered extensive drone damage in recent weeks, causing widespread power blackouts across Sudan.

Concurrently, the report documented an official diplomatic briefing convened in Port Sudan by the Sudanese **[[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Sudan)]]** in coordination with state security services. Undersecretary Ambassador **[[Muawiya Osman]]** briefed accredited foreign diplomatic missions, formally accusing the **[[Government of Ethiopia]]** and the **[[United Arab Emirates]]** of conducting a proxy drone war against Sudan. Osman specifically alleged that the **[[Assosa]]** (`أصوصا`) military base in Ethiopia serves as a staging hub for transferring weapons, equipment, and foreign mercenaries to the RSF and as a launch platform for long-range drone strikes penetrating eastern Sudan and targeting critical national infrastructure.
