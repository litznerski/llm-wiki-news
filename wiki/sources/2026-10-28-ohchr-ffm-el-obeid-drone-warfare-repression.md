## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 28 October 2026, the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]**, established by the UN Human Rights Council and operating under [[OHCHR]], published a major investigative report titled *"Sudan's Unrelenting War: Civilian Devastation and Deepening Insecurity in El Obeid"* (A/HRC/65/CRP.1). 

The press release details how the strategic capital of [[North Kordofan]] (`SD13`), **[[El Obeid]]** (`SD13001`), with approximately 600,000 residents, has been placed under partial encirclement and repeated unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) bombardment by the **[[Rapid Support Forces]]** (RSF). The RSF drone attacks have systematically targeted residential neighborhoods, schools, IDP displacement sites, and critical infrastructure (electricity networks, water pumping stations, and healthcare facilities), acts the Mission assesses may constitute war crimes. Concurrently, the Mission documented that the **[[Sudanese Armed Forces]]** (SAF) and allied military intelligence have deployed inside civilian neighborhoods—heightening foreseeable risks of attack—while enforcing intense state surveillance, mass arbitrary arrests, torture in unofficial detention cells, and incommunicado imprisonment in El Obeid Central Prison, severely constricting civic space. 

The Mission's leadership—Chair [[Mohamed Chande Othman]], Expert Member [[Mona Rishmawi]], and Expert Member [[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]]—further linked the El Obeid crisis to ongoing drone atrocities in [[Blue Nile State]], the 19 October drone massacre in [[Abyei]], and strikes near the Sudan-Egypt border killing artisanal miners, tracing these capabilities to transnational arms networks fueling the belligerents with advanced UAV systems.
