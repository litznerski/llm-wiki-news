## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that the Coordination Council of the **[[Emergency Response Rooms]]** in [[El Malha]] (`SD02008`) locality, northern [[North Darfur]], sounded the alarm over a catastrophic convergence of acute drought, widespread agricultural failure, complete exhaustion of food grain reserves, and an acute liquidity cash crunch.

The humanitarian council reported that pastoralist communities have suffered a collapse in livestock prices—their primary economic asset—compounded by bank closures and currency scarcity that has decimated household purchasing power. Thousands of destitute families have engaged in forced displacement from northern pastoral tracts into El Malha town, where families are sheltering under trees and in open fields without food rations as winter temperatures arrive. The council appealed for immediate life-saving food and shelter assistance.
