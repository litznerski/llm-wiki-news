## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 31 October 2026, **[[Arab News]]**, syndicating wire dispatch reporting from **[[Agence France-Presse]]** (AFP), reported that United Nations Secretary-General **[[António Guterres]]** expressed grave alarm and was "appalled" by the catastrophic toll of escalating drone strikes across Sudan. The report provided crucial evidentiary specificity to the UN Secretary-General's communiqué, explicitly identifying a mass-casualty drone strike executed by the **[[Sudanese Armed Forces]]** (SAF) on Monday, 27 October 2026, targeting **Tanqonj Market** in **[[Nyala]]** (`SD03001`), the capital of **[[South Darfur]]** (`SD03`).

According to the dispatch, the SAF airstrike on the crowded market killed **at least 102 civilians** and injured dozens more. Nyala serves as the administrative, economic, and military stronghold of the **[[Rapid Support Forces]]** (RSF) in Darfur, where the paramilitary organization has established a parallel civil administration competing with the Port Sudan-based government. UN Spokesperson **[[Stéphane Dujarric]]** condemned the escalating strikes as blatant violations of international humanitarian law, calling upon both the SAF and the RSF to immediately spare civilians and vital infrastructure and agree without delay to a cessation of hostilities.
