## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

The official portal of the **[[Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan]]** (FFM Sudan) hosted on the [[OHCHR]] domain documents the legislative mandate, leadership composition, investigative protocols, and comprehensive dossier archive of the primary UN accountability mechanism for the Sudan conflict. 

Established on 11 October 2023 by the **[[UN Human Rights Council]]** via resolution **A/HRC/RES/54/2**, and extended through October 2027 by resolution **A/HRC/63/L.26**, the Mission is tasked with investigating all alleged violations and abuses of human rights and international humanitarian law committed by the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]], the [[Rapid Support Forces]], and allied warring factions since 15 April 2023. The Mission is comprised of three independent international experts: **[[Mohamed Chande Othman]]** (Chair, former Chief Justice of Tanzania), **[[Joy Ngozi Ezeilo]]** (Senior Advocate of Nigeria and law professor), and **[[Mona Rishmawi]]** (former senior UN human rights official and jurist). 

The portal serves as the authoritative repository for the Mission's landmark reports:
1. *"Sudan's Unrelenting War: Civilian Devastation and Deepening Insecurity in El Obeid"* (A/HRC/65/CRP.1, 28 October 2026);
2. *"Fuelling Sudan's Conflict: Weapons, Fighters and External Support Networks"* (A/HRC/63/23, September 2026);
3. *"Information Supplementing the Report 'Sudan: Hallmarks of Genocide in El Fasher'"* (A/HRC/61/CRP.2, February 2026);
4. Chair [[Mohamed Chande Othman]]'s oral updates to the UN Human Rights Council and address to the UN General Assembly Third Committee on 28 October 2026.
