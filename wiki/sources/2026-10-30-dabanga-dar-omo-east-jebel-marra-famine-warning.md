## 1. Source Metadata & 5W1H Provenance Anchor

- **Who**: Native administration leader [[Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah]] (Shartai of Dar Omo) and displacement committee director [[Adam Yagoub Mohamed Abdallah]]; investigated by [[Suleiman Sirri]] for [[Radio Dabanga]].
- **What**: Acute humanitarian emergency and imminent famine alert across the 8 federations of Dar Omo (`دائرة دار أمو`) in East Jebel Marra. The area suffered a 95% agricultural failure ("white autumn" / `خريف أبيض` with only 5% rainfall coverage), total shutdown of its sole health center serving 30 villages for 10 months, contaminated drinking water sources, school dropouts, and an influx of thousands of displaced persons fleeing El Fasher and Shangil Tobaya.
- **Where**: [[Dar Omo]], [[Liba]], and East Jebel Marra in the [[Jebel Marra]] massif, controlled by the [[Sudan Liberation Movement (al-Nur)]] (SLM-AW).
- **When**: 30 October 2026.
- **Why**: Combination of severe climate-induced drought, total commercial and humanitarian blockade surrounding Darfur conflict zones, and complete cessation of international aid operations on the ground.

## 3. Bilingual Direct Citations & Forensic Evidence

> [!QUOTE] Shartai Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah Statement (`2026-10-30`)
> **Original Arabic**:
> "فدرالية دار أمو التي تضم 8 فدراليات وتقع تحت سيطرة حركة جيش تحرير السودان، تعاني من أوضاع إنسانية سيئة في كل المناطق التابعة لها، داية من بلي، سورو، كيلا، ليبة كدنير، فينا، كارا، لوقودلو... المنطقة مازالت تستقبل موجات نزوح جديدة من شتى ولايات السودان، لكنهم لم يجدوا أي خدمة متوفرة."
> **English Translation**:
> "The federation of Dar Omo, which comprises 8 federations and is under the control of the Sudan Liberation Movement/Army, suffers from dire humanitarian conditions across all its subordinate areas, beginning from Bale, Soro, Kila, Liba Kadneer, Feina, Kara, Lugudlu... The area continues to receive new waves of displacement from various states of Sudan, but they have found no available services whatsoever."
> — **[[Ibrahim Suleiman Hasaballah]]**, Shartai of Dar Omo Circle (`2026-10-30`)

> [!QUOTE] Adam Yagoub Mohamed Abdallah Statement (`2026-10-30`)
> **Original Arabic**:
> "لقد بلغت نسبة الأمطار والتغطية المائية نحو 5% فقط، وهي نسبة لا تسمح بتحقيق أي إنتاج زراعي على أرض الواقع لتلبية احتياجات أهالي المنطقة... خريف هذا العام خريف أبيض ولا يوجد فيه أي إعاشة وفاشل بنسبة كبيرة... المنطقة لا يوجد بها سوى مركز صحي واحد يقدم خدماته لنحو 30 قرية مجاورة... إلا أن هذا المركز أصبح خارج إطار الخدمة منذ أكثر من 10 أشهر."
> **English Translation**:
> "The rainfall and water coverage reached only about 5%, a percentage that does not allow for any real agricultural production on the ground to meet the needs of the area's residents... This year's autumn was a 'white autumn', with no livelihood yield, and failed to a massive extent... The area has only a single health center providing services to around 30 neighboring villages... yet this center has been out of service for over 10 months."
> — **[[Adam Yagoub Mohamed Abdallah]]**, Displaced Persons and Organizations Committee Head, Liba Federation (`2026-10-30`)
