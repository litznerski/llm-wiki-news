## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] covered the concluding ceremonies of the Pink October breast cancer awareness month at **[[Khartoum Oncology Hospital]]** (widely known as *Mustashfa al-Dharra* / `مستشفى الذرة`), Sudan’s principal specialized public cancer treatment center. Hospital Director-General **[[Ahmed Omar]]** emphasized the extraordinary operational hurdles confronting medical staff, including acute electrical outages resulting from drone strikes on the Khartoum grid, complete absence of radiotherapy equipment, and catastrophic shortages of diagnostic reagents.

Cancer survivor and activist **[[Lamia Mutawakil]]** launched an urgent appeal for community and international funding to procure a linear accelerator radiotherapy machine, warning that awareness campaigns are meaningless without functional oncology hardware. Broadcaster Abdallah Mohammed al-Hassan announced an initiative through *Bank al-Thawab* to support the radiotherapy machine and sponsor a daily hospital soup kitchen (*takiyya*) to feed patients and medical staff.
