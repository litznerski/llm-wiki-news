## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that the **[[National Umma Party]]** branch in [[River Nile State]] (`SD04`) sounded an urgent alarm warning of imminent famine across the state. The party declared the winter agricultural season (2026–2027) on the verge of complete collapse due to the acute disappearance of diesel fuel (gasoline/gasoil) for over three weeks at filling stations, coupled with rolling electrical power outages reaching up to **18 hours daily** following drone attacks on the power grid.

The Umma Party formally petitioned the Governor of River Nile State, the Minister of Agriculture, and the State Security Committee to immediately declare a **state of "agricultural emergency."** The party demanded the emergency reallocation of strategic national petroleum reserves to agricultural pump schemes, an immediate exemption for farming projects from electricity load shedding, and the waiver of exorbitant state fees and taxes on seed and agricultural inputs, warning that the state's hosting of hundreds of thousands of IDPs makes a food gap catastrophic.
