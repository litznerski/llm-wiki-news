## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported displacement telemetry published by the **[[International Organization for Migration]]** (IOM) Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM), documenting that over **2,000 civilians** were forcibly displaced across northern [[North Darfur]] between 29 October and 1 November 2026 due to severe security deterioration and armed militia attacks.

On Saturday, 31 October 2026, approximately **850 individuals** fled the rural villages of "Kamo" (`كامو`) and "Touki" (`توكي`) in **[[Karnoi]]** (`SD02007`) locality, relocating to safer areas within Karnoi and Tina localities, with several dozen crossing into Chad. Concurrently, between 29 and 31 October 2026, an estimated **1,150 individuals** were displaced from four rural villages across **[[Um Baru]]** (`SD02006`) locality, fleeing either into central Um Baru or across the international border toward Chadian refugee reception centers.
