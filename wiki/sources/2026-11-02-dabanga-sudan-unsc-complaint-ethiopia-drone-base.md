## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 2 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that the Sudanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs (**[[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Sudan)]]**) officially lodged simultaneous formal complaints against the **[[Government of Ethiopia]]** with both the **United Nations Security Council** and the **African Union Peace and Security Council**. The complaint formally charges Addis Ababa with direct complicity in hostile cross-border acts that violate Article 2, Paragraph 4 of the United Nations Charter prohibiting the threat or use of force against the territorial integrity of any state.

The Sudanese government submitted evidentiary dossiers and flight tracking telemetry alleging that the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) are utilizing Ethiopian territory—specifically the **[[Assosa]]** military base—as a staging and launch platform for suicide drones and missile-armed UAVs penetrating deep into eastern, central, and northern Sudan. The complaint detailed strikes disabling **14 electrical power distribution stations** across nine states (Gedaref, Khartoum, Al Jazirah, Red Sea, Sennar, White Nile, South Kordofan, River Nile, and Northern State), resulting in civilian deaths and nationwide utility blackouts. The complaint demanded that the international community enforce international law against Ethiopia.
