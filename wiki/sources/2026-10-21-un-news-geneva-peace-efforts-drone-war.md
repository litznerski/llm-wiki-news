## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 21 October 2026, **[[UN News]]** reported on high-level multilateral talks convened in **[[Geneva]]**, Switzerland, officially known as the *Consultative Meetings on Enhancing Coordination of Peace Initiatives and Efforts in Sudan*. Co-hosted by the **[[United Nations]]** and the **[[Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (Switzerland)]]**, the meeting brought together delegations from nearly two dozen nations, alongside the "Quad" (United States, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt) and "Quintet" (African Union, IGAD, UN, EU, Arab League) mediation tracks, as well as humanitarian organizations including the ICRC and the Sudan INGO Forum. Neither the Sudanese Armed Forces nor the Rapid Support Forces attended directly, as the session focused on synchronizing external mediation efforts.

Swiss State Secretary **[[Alexandre Fasel]]** issued a sharp assessment, stating: *"The war in Sudan has become a drone war; it kills mostly civilians... Famine-like conditions persist in Darfur, the Kordofan state and elsewhere in the country; it is the war that drives this hunger."* Fasel stressed that under the Geneva Conventions, the protection of non-combatants and humanitarian access cannot wait for a formal political settlement. Personal Envoy of the UN Secretary-General for Sudan **[[Pekka Haavisto]]** called upon international partners to utilize their bilateral channels to build confidence, de-escalate violence, and foster conditions conducive to a comprehensive ceasefire and a civilian-led transitional dialogue.
