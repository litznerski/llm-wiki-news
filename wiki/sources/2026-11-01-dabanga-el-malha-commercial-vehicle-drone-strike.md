## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that an armed drone struck a civilian commercial transport vehicle on the outskirts of the "Al-Hara" (`الحارة`) area in [[El Malha]] (`SD02008`) locality, northern [[North Darfur]]. The attack occurred at approximately 14:00 Sudan time on Sunday, 1 November 2026, targeting a vehicle ferrying civilian passengers and merchandise to the local weekly market.

According to the **[[Darfur Victims Support Organization]]** (`منظمة مناصرة ضحايا دارفور`), the strike resulted in the immediate death of two named civilians: **Muhammad Musa Bakheit Tikras** (`محمد موسى بخيت تكراس`) and **Ali Ismail Abakar** (`علي إسماعيل أبكر`), with several additional passengers sustaining wounds. The organization condemned the ongoing targeting of civilian transport networks along remote desert supply corridors connecting northern Darfur to Kufra and northern transit routes.
