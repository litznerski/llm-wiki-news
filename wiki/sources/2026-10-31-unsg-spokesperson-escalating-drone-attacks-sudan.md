## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On **31 October 2026**, [[Stéphane Dujarric]], Spokesperson for United Nations Secretary-General [[António Guterres]], delivered an official statement via the [[Office of the Spokesperson for the Secretary-General]] ([[United Nations]]) strongly condemning the escalating unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV / drone) attacks across Sudan and the disputed [[Abyei]] area by the belligerent parties to the conflict—namely the [[Sudanese Armed Forces]] (SAF) and the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]], [[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]).

Key forensic, humanitarian, and diplomatic determinations in the communiqué include:
1. **Mass Civilian Toll & Geographic Scope**: Across Sudan as well as in demilitarized [[Abyei]] (`SD18001`), drone strikes by the parties to the conflict have killed **hundreds of civilians**, including women and children, corroborating the `306+` three-week death toll documented earlier on 23 October 2026 by [[OHCHR]] and [[UNISFA]] ([[2026-10 Sudan Drone Warfare Escalation]], [[2026-10-19 Abyei Amiet Market and Todach Drone Strike]]).
2. **International Humanitarian Law (IHL) Violations & Health Facility Targeting**: The Secretary-General explicitly determined that these drone attacks killing civilians across Sudan and [[Abyei]] **constitute violations of international humanitarian law (IHL)**, expressing outrage at attacks directed against civilians and critical infrastructure, specifically **health facilities** ([[Drone Proliferation and Double-Tap Strike Doctrine in Sudan]]).
3. **Demand for Immediate Cessation of Hostilities & Good-Faith Mediation**: [[António Guterres]] called on all conflict parties to spare civilians and critical infrastructure, agree without delay to an immediate **cessation of hostilities** as a first step toward ending the war, and engage in good faith with mediation efforts, reaffirming [[United Nations]] readiness to support a sustainable political resolution reflecting the aspirations of the Sudanese people.
