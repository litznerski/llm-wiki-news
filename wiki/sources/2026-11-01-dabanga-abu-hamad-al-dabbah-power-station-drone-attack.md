## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that the [[Rapid Support Forces]] (RSF) launched coordinated early-morning drone strikes targeting key electrical power infrastructure and military command installations across northern Sudan. In [[River Nile State]] (`SD04`), an unmanned aerial vehicle hit the central electricity transformer station in [[Abu Hamad]] (`SD04006`), triggering complete municipal power failure following three heavy detonations. Concurrently in [[Northern State]] (`SD05`), an RSF drone struck the main power distribution station in [[Al Dabbah]] (`SD05004`), disabling the facility and plunging the district into a total blackout.

The attack on Al Dabbah also targeted the command headquarters of the SAF 73rd Infantry Brigade and two fuel stations in the "Al-Multaqa" (`المتلقى`) sector. The strikes inflicted casualties among military personnel, including the verified death of **[[Muhammad al-Nao]]** (`محمد النو`), the prominent local commander of the Islamist **[[Al-Baraa Ibn Malik Brigade]]** allied with the SAF. The Dongola Resistance Committees condemned the attack for paralyzing civilian services and essential utilities. The strikes brought the total number of power stations hit nationwide over a seven-day period to ten.
