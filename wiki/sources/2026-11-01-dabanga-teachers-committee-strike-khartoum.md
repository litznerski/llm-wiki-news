## 1. 5W1H Source Intelligence Summary & Provenance

On 1 November 2026, [[Radio Dabanga]] reported that the **[[Sudanese Teachers Committee]]** announced the continuation of its intermittent general strike for the third and fourth consecutive weeks across [[Khartoum State]] (`SD01`). The strike schedule designated Wednesday and Thursday of the current week, followed by Monday and Tuesday of the following week, as full work stoppages.

The strike, initially launched two weeks prior, was organized to demand an increase in the statutory minimum wage, the full settlement of accumulated salary arrears going back months, and a binding state commitment to finance public education. The Higher Strike Committee declared active participation in the labor stoppage a moral and professional duty to defend the dignity of the teaching profession and ensure the survival of public educational institutions amidst wartime state abandonment.
