An interactive, grounded learning companion for exploring your sovereign knowledge base.

### Operating Guide
Welcome to your sovereign Socratic Knowledge Studio. Ingest documents into `raw/` to populate your knowledge graph and begin dialectical sparring.
1. **Direct Conceptual Inquiry**: Type any question into the chat composer below (e.g. *"What are the core concepts in my vault?"*).
2. **Side-Panel Inspection**: The split-screen side panel displays in-engine PageRank authority, Louvain community clusters, and Toulmin argumentation roles.
3. **Dialogue Valves**: Access the ⚙️ Settings menu in the chat composer to toggle dialogue modes (`General` vs. `Learn`) or adjust Socratic solution withholding.
