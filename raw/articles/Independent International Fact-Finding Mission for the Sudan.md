![Nicaragua map](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/2023-12/fffm-sudan_map.jpg) Responding to the human rights and humanitarian crisis caused by the ongoing armed conflict in the Sudan, the Human Rights Council, on 11 October 2023, through resolution [**A/HRC/RES/54/2**](https://eur02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fundocs.org%2FA%2FHRC%2FRES%2F54%2F2&data=05%7C02%7Cohchr-infodesign%40un.org%7C1c181c2840794df4962508de2d903389%7C0f9e35db544f4f60bdcc5ea416e6dc70%7C0%7C0%7C638998294516193288%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=7CWxq6gDuSxfBD95COcpMARoMJeET7plC0ruiBa69Rc%3D&reserved=0) decided to establish an independent international fact-finding mission for the Sudan to investigate and establish the facts, circumstances and root causes of all alleged human rights violations and abuses and violations of international humanitarian law, including those committed against refugees, and related crimes in the context of the ongoing armed conflict that began on 15 April 2023, between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces, as well as other warring parties. The Council extended the Fact-Finding Mission’s mandate for one year in October 2024 through resolution [**A/HRC/RES/57/2**](https://docs.un.org/A/HRC/RES/57/2), and for an additional year in October 2025 through resolution [**A/HRC/RES/60/3**](https://docs.un.org/en/A/HRC/RES/60/3), requesting the Mission to:

- Provide the Human Rights Council with an oral update on its work at its 62nd session (June-July 2026), to be followed by an enhanced interactive dialogue.
- Provide a comprehensive report at its 63rd session (September-October 2026), to be followed by an interactive dialogue.
- Present the report to the General Assembly at its 81 <sup>st</sup> session (October 2026), to be followed by an interactive dialogue.

During a special session of the Human Rights Council on the human rights situation in and around El Fasher, in the context of the ongoing conflict in the Sudan, held on 14 November 2025, the Council adopted resolution [A/HRC/RES/S-38/1](https://hrcmeetings.ohchr.org/HRCSessions/SpecialSessions/38/Documents/A_HRC_RES_S_38_1%20%20AdvanceEditedVersion.pdf "https://hrcmeetings.ohchr.org/hrcsessions/specialsessions/38/documents/a_hrc_res_s_38_1%20%20advanceeditedversion.pdf"), the Council requesting the Fact-Finding Mission to:

- Conduct an urgent inquiry into the recent violations and abuses of international human rights law and violations of international humanitarian law allegedly committed in and around El Fasher;
- Identify, where possible, all those for whom there are reasonable grounds to believe that they are responsible for alleged violations and abuses of international human rights law in and around El Fasher and to support efforts to ensure that the perpetrators of alleged abuses and violations are held accountable;
- Give due regard to ensuring the complementarity of its efforts with those of other actors, including independent national accountability efforts and regional investigations;
- Present a report on the findings of its inquiry to the Council at its 61st session (February-April 2026), followed by an enhanced interactive dialogue.

On 6 July 2026, resolution A/HRC/62/L.48 was adopted following an urgent debate on the situation in El Obeid, held before the Human Rights Council on 3 July 2026. This resolution:

- Requests the Fact-Finding Mission to conduct, within its existing resources, an urgent inquiry into any violations and abuses of international human rights law and violations of international humanitarian law and related international crimes, allegedly committed in and around El Obeid in the context of the ongoing conflict in the Sudan;
- Encourages the fact-finding mission to update the Human Rights Council on this inquiry during the interactive dialogue at its sixty-third session and to update the General Assembly during the interactive dialogue at its eighty-first session.

## Mandate

In accordance with Human Rights Council resolution [A/HRC/RES/54/2](https://undocs.org/A/HRC/RES/54/2) the Fact-Finding mission is provided with the following mandate:

1. To investigate and establish the facts, circumstances and root causes of all alleged human rights violations and abuses and violations of international humanitarian law, including those committed against refugees, and related crimes in the context of the ongoing armed conflict that began on 15 April 2023, between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces, as well as other warring parties;
2. To collect, consolidate and analyse evidence of such violations and abuses, including those affecting women and children, and to systematically record and preserve all information, documentation and evidence, including interviews, witness testimony and forensic material, consistent with international best practices, in view of any future legal proceedings;
3. To document and verify relevant information and evidence, including through field engagement, and to cooperate with judicial and other entities, as appropriate;
4. To identify, where possible, those individuals and entities responsible for violations or abuses of human rights or violations of international humanitarian law, or other related crimes, in the Sudan, with a view to ensuring that those responsible are held accountable;
5. To make recommendations, in particular on accountability measures, all with a view to ending impunity and addressing its root causes, and ensuring accountability, including, as appropriate, individual criminal responsibility, and access to justice for victims;
6. To cooperate and share best practice with other international, regional and domestic accountability initiatives, as and when these are established, as appropriate;
7. To cooperate and share best practice with other international, regional and domestic accountability initiatives, as and when these are established, as appropriate;

## Cooperation

The Human Rights Council, through resolutions 54/2 and 57/2 and 60/3, has requested the Fact-Finding Mission, with due consideration of distinctive mandates and practicability, to coordinate, cooperate, ensure complementarity of efforts with, and draw on the expertise of a range of stakeholders. In the implementation of its mandate, the Fact-Finding Mission therefore welcomes cooperation with all stakeholders in furthering its mandate, including civil society organizations. To this end, the Fact-Finding Mission has developed a *Terms of Reference and Guidance for Civil Society Organizations and Other Stakeholders seeking to Cooperate with the Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan*. Its Secretariat is also available for further inquiries through email at [ffmsudan@un.org](mailto:ffmsudan@un.org).

## Call for Submissions

The Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan invites all interested individuals, groups and organizations to submit information and documentation of relevance to any violations, abuses and related crimes committed in the context of the ongoing armed conflict that began on 15 April 2023, between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces, as well as other warring parties, in particular on recent violations and abuses allegedly committed. It appreciates receiving relevant information and documentation at any time, however, for consideration during the current reporting cycle the deadline for submissions is **31 July 2026**.

All interested parties are requested to read through the call for submissions and complete the submission form for each submission to be sent to the Fact-Finding Mission. All submissions should be sent to [**submissions-ffmsudan@un.org**](mailto:submissions-ffmsudan@un.org "mailto:submissions-ffmsudan@un.org"). For alternative submission arrangements for sensitive information and all queries, please contact the Secretariat by email at [**ffmsudan@un.org**](mailto:ffmsudan@un.org "mailto:ffmsudan@un.org").

Please follow [this link](https://www.ohchr.org/en/hr-bodies/hrc/ffm-sudan/call-for-submissions "FFM SUDAN - URGENT CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS  MANDATE REPORT SEPTEMBER 2026") for more information.
