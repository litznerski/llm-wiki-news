Sudan’s civil war has triggered the world's worst humanitarian crisis. Tens of thousands have been killed and millions displaced, with widespread allegations of ethnic cleansing and acts amounting to genocide. Despite this, the country has been largely forgotten by the international community amid conflicts in the Middle East and Ukraine.
